        Amateur radio is about service. MMARC is dedicated to serving Jefferson City and Cole County through emergency communications, public events, and community education.
    </p>

    <h2>Public Event Communications</h2>
    <p>
        Planning a marathon, 5K, bike rally, or community parade? Coordinating logistics over a wide area or difficult terrain can be challenging. 
    </p>
    <p><strong>How We Can Help:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Reliable Coverage:</strong> We provide robust, reliable communications support using our network of repeaters, portable radios, and mobile stations.</li>
        <li><strong>End-to-End Coordination:</strong> Our volunteer operators ensure that race organizers, rest stops, and emergency medical personnel stay connected from start to finish.</li>
    </ul>

    <hr>

    <h2>Emergency Communications</h2>
    <p>
        When conventional systems fail due to severe weather or civil emergencies, MMARC members are dedicated to providing reliable communication links.
    </p>

    <h3>🌪️ Skywarn</h3>
    <p>
        <a href="https://www.weather.gov/SKYWARN" target="_blank" class="text-brand-600 hover:text-brand-800 dark:text-brand-400 dark:hover:text-brand-300">SKYWARN®</a> is a volunteer program established by the National Weather Service. 
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>The Mission:</strong> Our members act as trained severe weather spotters, observing and reporting critical conditions locally.</li>
        <li><strong>The Impact:</strong> During severe storms, MMARC operators activate our local nets to relay real-time weather information directly to the NWS, helping them issue accurate, timely warnings that save lives.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>🚑 Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARES)</h3>
    <p>
        The Amateur Radio Emergency Service® (ARES) consists of licensed amateurs who have voluntarily registered their qualifications and equipment for communications duty in the public service. 
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>The Mission:</strong> We work closely with the Cole County Emergency Management Agency, local hospitals, and first responders to provide backup communications during crises.</li>
    </ul>

    <hr>

    <h2>Education and Presentations</h2>
    <p>
        Are you looking for an engaging presentation for your community organization, club, or school? 
    </p>
    <p><strong>What We Offer:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Engaging presentations on the science of radio waves and the history of communications.</li>
        <li>Live demonstrations of modern amateur radio technology in action.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>⚜️ Scout Merit Badges</h3>
    <p>
        We actively partner with local Scout troops to help them earn the <strong>Radio Merit Badge</strong>. 
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Hands-On Learning:</strong> Our members provide hands-on demonstrations and coordinate educational activities.</li>
        <li><strong>Practical Skills:</strong> We help scouts build their first wire antennas.</li>
        <li><strong>On The Air:</strong> Best of all, we get them on the air to talk with other amateur radio operators around the country and the world!</li>
    </ul>
