## Public Event Communications

Planning a marathon, 5K, bike rally, or community parade? Coordinating logistics over a wide area or difficult terrain can be challenging. 

**How We Can Help:**
* **Reliable Coverage:** We provide robust, reliable communications support using our network of repeaters, portable radios, and mobile stations. 
* **End-to-End Coordination:** Our volunteer operators ensure that race organizers, rest stops, and emergency medical personnel stay connected from start to finish.

---

## Emergency Communications

When conventional systems fail due to severe weather or civil emergencies, MMARC members are dedicated to providing reliable communication links.

### 🌪️ Skywarn
[SKYWARN®](https://www.weather.gov/SKYWARN) is a volunteer program established by the National Weather Service. 

* **The Mission:** Our members act as trained severe weather spotters, observing and reporting critical conditions locally. 
* **The Impact:** During severe storms, MMARC operators activate our local nets to relay real-time weather information directly to the NWS, helping them issue accurate, timely warnings that save lives.

### 🚑 Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARES)
The Amateur Radio Emergency Service® (ARES) consists of licensed amateurs who have voluntarily registered their qualifications and equipment for communications duty in the public service. 

* **The Mission:** We work closely with the Cole County Emergency Management Agency, local hospitals, and first responders to provide backup communications during crises.

## Education and Presentations

Are you looking for an engaging presentation for your community organization, club, or school? 

**What We Offer:**
* Engaging presentations on the science of radio waves and the history of communications.
* Live demonstrations of modern amateur radio technology in action.

### ⚜️ Scout Merit Badges

We actively partner with local Scout troops to help them earn the **Radio Merit Badge**. 

* **Hands-On Learning:** Our members provide hands-on demonstrations and coordinate educational activities.
* **Practical Skills:** We help scouts build their first wire antennas.
* **On The Air:** Best of all, we get them on the air to talk with other amateur radio operators around the country and the world!
