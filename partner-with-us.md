## Public Event Communications

Planning a marathon, 5K, bike rally, or community parade? Coordinating logistics over a wide area or difficult terrain can be challenging. Our club can provide robust, reliable communications support for your public events. Using our network of repeaters, portable radios, and mobile stations, our volunteer operators ensure that race organizers, rest stops, and emergency medical personnel stay connected from start to finish. 

## Emergency Communications

MMARC members are dedicated to providing reliable communication links when conventional systems fail due to severe weather or civil emergencies. 

### Skywarn
[SKYWARN®](https://www.weather.gov/SKYWARN) is a volunteer program established by the National Weather Service. Our members act as trained severe weather spotters, observing and reporting critical conditions locally. During severe storms, MMARC operators activate our local nets to relay real-time weather information directly to the NWS, helping them issue accurate, timely warnings that save lives.

### Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARES)
The Amateur Radio Emergency Service® (ARES) consists of licensed amateurs who have voluntarily registered their qualifications and equipment for communications duty in the public service. We work closely with the Cole County Emergency Management Agency, local hospitals, and first responders to provide backup communications during crises. 

## Education and Presentations

Are you looking for an engaging presentation for your community organization, club, or school? MMARC offers presentations on the science of radio waves, the history of communications, and modern amateur radio technology.

### Scout Merit Badges
We actively partner with local Scout troops to help them earn the **Radio Merit Badge**. Our members can provide hands-on demonstrations, coordinate activities, help scouts build their first antennas, and get them on the air to talk with other amateur radio operators around the world!
