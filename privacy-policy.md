# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** {{ site.time | date: "%B %d, %Y" }}  
*(Originally drafted: April 9, 2026)*

The Mid-Missouri Amateur Radio Club (MMARC) respects your privacy. We only gather basic information such as your IP address and browser details for use in standard server logging and website analytics. This helps us ensure the website is functioning correctly and allows us to understand how our site is utilized by visitors.

We **do not** sell, rent, or otherwise share your data with any third parties.

Please also note that by its very nature, amateur radio is an open and transparent hobby. Communication over the airwaves is public and can be heard by anyone with appropriate equipment. 

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices or how we handle your information, please do not hesitate to contact our club officers.
