K0ETY Repeater Report:
        Mike Dolson, N0ZH, reporting: The new antenna has been installed and the two repeaters are
        linked. There is an anti-kerchunking setting so you must hold the PTT button for about a
        second before talking. The phone service will be disconnected shortly. There is no electric bill
        for the repeater at St. Mary’s Hospital. There is no longer a CW “P” as a courtesy tone. It has
        been replaced by beeps – one means the repeaters are not linked; two means they are linked.
        Further, low-pitched beeps indicate the person is transmitting on 147.00 and high-pitched
        beeps indicate 442.150. Users can link & unlink but in the near future, the system will return
        to linked at midnight. The phone patch is no longer functional.

ARES Report:
        J.D. Simmons, WA0BER, reporting. Area counties have been fairly active. The Montgomery
        County EC has stepped down due to health issues. The new Callaway County EC is in the
        process of getting approved. I went to a meeting in Mexico, MO last week with about 20
        attendees. The meeting involved telling the Emergency Management Director what ham radio
        operators could do for his agency. Regions A & F had a joint drill at the state fairgrounds in
        Sedalia with several area EMT groups participating. The drill was worked through the
        Boonville repeater which worked very well. There were some problems with HF, however.
        Jerry Sill, NN0JS, added that the main focus is to rebuild the served agency liaison network.
