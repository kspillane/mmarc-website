2 Meter Report:
          Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Operational. Work was conducted on the VHF repeater last
          week. The duplexer was well out of tune. With that correction, we observed no reflected power and
          a considerable improvement in forward power. Nothing new on the UHF repeater.

Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN):
          Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Operational.

Digipeater Report:
           Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Not working currently. Rick Bennett, KC0PET, will check on

           Bill Hudson, W0WFH, not present.

CW Class:
            Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, and Bill Gerth, W4RK, reporting: We have a new beginner who started last
            month; first name is Karl. We have been practicing with straight keys because of Straight Key Night
            coming up December 31st. Kent also talked about silent key, Ron Casteel.

Training Committee:
          Mike Thayer, KM0S, reporting: Technician exams scheduled for class members this coming Monday.
          Others are welcome to test at the same time.

Old Business:

            1. The floor was opened for nominations for all officer positions. As none were forthcoming, the
               slate nominated at the November meeting (all current officers) was reelected for a second term.
            2. Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, will complete his term as Director at the end of the year and
               nominations were again opened for his successor. None were forthcoming and Kent Trimble,
               nominated at the November meeting was elected to fill the position for a three year term.

         None

           5. Eagle Days at Lake of the Ozarks will have a special event station operating. This will be in
