2-Meter Repeater report: Tom, NØSS, reported that the repeater is working ok. Tom and
Jim, NØTKN, will make a trip to check the power output, vacuum and clean the repeater,

digipeater. There will be work at the site on Friday, March 11 . Some coax is hanging

Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported the system is
working fine. The Jefferson City site is going through a major overhaul. There was a
change in the power service and new ground system was put in.

Emergency Coordinator's report: Rich, NØJBF, reported that the weather spotter classes
have stalled out. Mike, NØZH, added that money is running low so there may not be more
classes.

Rich, NØJBF, and Tom, NØSS, attended a debriefing for the January exercise and went
over the afteraction report. Everyone was pleased with the way the amateur radio operators
performed. There is an education process needed for those who plan future exercises.

Joe, KBØQHZ, will be sending out information he received from the Section Coordinator
with his ideas on the afteraction report.

VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported that there were two exams last month and
both passed. There will be four or five exams following the meeting.

CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reported that the advanced class is has experienced a little
lower turnout but is still going well. Currently, they are playing with a contest simulator.
This class meets at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings.

Kent, K9ZTV, reported the intermediate and beginning classes are also going well. The
instructors have been sending messages to one person while other class members simulate
QRM. The intermediate class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am,
while the beginning class meets an hour later at 10am.

Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, talked with John Campbell and with the upcoming
exercise, time for planning, and the time required for the Tech class, it was decided that the
Tech class would be better suited for later this fall. The General class will be proceed
beginning near the end of the month. There will be about 10 sessions and should finish
prior to Field Day.

Mike added that there are resources from the ARRL to aid in teaching the classes. One of
these is the Modulation and Wave Fundamentals Board. Tom, NØSS, moved that the club
purchase this board and the motion passed.

Kent, K9ZTV, moved that the club purchase books for the instructors. Rich, NØJBF,
amended the motion to include purchasing the books with the instructor discount. Steve,
KDØGRM, seconds, and the motion passed.
