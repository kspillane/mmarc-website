VE Liaison’s Report: - Mike Smith

I had one request for a test session, and it was fulfilled at Oscar's Classic Diner on Saturday, 24
July. Kole Tilson, KD0RFN, upgraded from Technician to General. Test sessions should be
able to continue with advance notice in association with our periodic meetings.

I keep a list of known Volunteer Examiners (VE), though some have not proctored an exam in a
long time so may be considered inactive by the ARRL. The process to become a volunteer
examiner is straightforward and can be done at one's own pace. The following link has
information about how to renew your VE status or obtain VE
credentials. See: http://www.arrl.org/become-an-arrl-ve If you renew/obtain VE credentials,
let me know so I can include you in future calls for an exam.
