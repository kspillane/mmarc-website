K0ETY Repeater Report:
        Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Both repeaters are linked and are working. We still have
        some issues with reception on the VHF repeater. Jim explained our repeater system and

          Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Working well.

Digipeater Report:
           Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Working well.

ARES Report:
        Jerry Sill, NN0JS, EC, reporting: There was an exercise on November 14th which J.D.
        Simmons, WA0BER, District F DEC, was happy with. It took a lot of preparation. 17 Hams
        participated and we believe everyone had a good time. There were some issues with Simplex
        (repeaters were not used) and also some issues with handhelds. The exercise ran about an
        hour. The message timing needs some work, but it was not bad. Traffic handling could use
        some training – there was a lot of talking over each other. The SEMA operator was working
        alone and was somewhat overworked. We need headset mics and two operators at each
        position. Next time, we should use mobiles instead of handhelds and we need to pre-determine
        locations. This was our first exercise of this type in a long time. Karl Schulte, WA2KBZ,
        added that he was donating and installing a radio at the Salvation Army.
