VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported that there will be a testing session following
the meeting for each class of license. New VE’s are especially invited to participate to learn
how to fill out the forms. Also, responses to email requests for help administering exams
are getting better, but could still use improvement.

As a reminder, if you are a VE and have recently upgraded your license and/or address,
please notify the ARRL VEC so they can send you the proper credentials for your new
license class. Keep in mind that the VEC maintains a separate database from the ARRL
general membership so make updates to both places.
