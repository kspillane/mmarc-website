Training Committee:
   Mike Thayer, KM0S, reporting – There will be a General Class coming up mid to late March.
   We need instructor support and I would like to have an Instructor meeting sometime prior to the
   class to discuss agenda and other items. There is no “canned” General course. If anyone can find
   a suitable course online, we’ll consider that. Otherwise, we may just use the Q & A manual for
   the General test. There is a chance I may not be able to attend all sessions. Class will take 8 to
   10 weeks with sessions on Monday evening. Club will provide books for the instructors;
   students will pay for theirs, which will be the only student cost.

          Mid-MO Emergency Radio Service:
             Rich Kreiser, N0JBF, reporting – had a discussion with Jerry. Need to have a meeting of

          Field Day 2013:
             Mike Dolson, N0ZH, reporting – Field Day rules changed: we can set up anytime after midnight
             Thursday. We might want to avoid the heat by setting up Friday evening. Questioned if we
             should have the porta-potties again this year – general opinion was yes. Was able to get the DPS
             vehicle this year; requires a CDL driver. We have at least three so that should not be an issue.
             Haven’t checked Fire Department vehicle yet. Not sure about Don Atkins’ camper as he is
             currently in Texas.

          SOTA:
            Bill Gerth, W4RK, reporting – wanted to bring Missouri into the SOTA (Summits on the Air)
            organization. Effective February 1, Missouri became an official SOTA association, one of 70 -
            80 around the world. Management in United Kingdom. An awards program for amateur radio. I
            was able to make 29 QSOs in 35 minutes; 3 were with MMARC members. I created a QSL card.
            Very first QSO from Taum Sauk mountain was Mike Dolson. There are 57 summits in Missouri;
            five have been activated so far. Bill built a website for the Missouri association; URL for the
            Missouri association is www.mosota.org

New Business:
             Discussion about the USS Missouri (BB63) which is moored in Pearl Harbor as a memorial.
             There is an active radio club there which puts the ship on the air frequently. The antenna has
             fallen into disrepair and the club there is seeking funds to repair it. Cost is $45,000.00 for
             needed parts. Details are available online. Karl Schulte, WA2KBZ, made a motion that we as a
             club send a donation of $150.00. Seconded by Bryan Norman, KD0GRU. Carried.

              Discussion about e-mailing minutes. A few copies will be printed for use at meeting. After
              discussion, motion was made and seconded to e-mail minutes. Carried.

              John Patterson, WC0W, is in Belize for CW contest. V31TP is call sign for contest. Also
