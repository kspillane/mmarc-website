2-Meter Repeater report: Tom, NØSS reported that the repeater is still working, however
the autopatch is still down.


Digipeater report: Rick, KCØPET, reported that the digipeater is alive and working again!
It is not at the site, but rather operating from Rick’s house. Tom donated a radio and TNC
to get it back up and running. More work needs to be done at the site. Tom moved that
Rick spend a reasonable amount of money for an enclosure to house the digipeater and the
motion passed.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that there have
been no updates. The site around Concordia is still being investigated.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ, reported that he and Rich attended a
planning meeting for the January SEMA exercise.

J.D., WAØBER, will be going to the bootheel region to talk to other EC’s about what will be
involved. The next planning meeting will be at the end of the month in Poplar Bluff. There
is a meeting next Saturday with 50-60 hams in District E to go over sites to be staffed.
Fifteen sites are envisioned when complete.

Rich, NØJBF, added that J.D. gave a very good presentation at the January planning
meeting on what can and cannot be done with ham radio with regard to exercise drills.


VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported that there are two exams to give after the
meeting. With help from Nancie, NMØC, the corrections and changes from the ARRL have
been incorporated into the exams. Last weekend Tom made two machine readable copies
of two different exams so that they can be loaded onto a laptop and read with Adobe
Acrobat. The exams can then be enlarged so that visually challenged examinees can better
read the questions/answers.

Please remember to respond to VE related emails if you are a current VE even if you
cannot help with upcoming exams.


CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reported that the advanced class is doing well despite the thin
turnouts recently. Students have been turning their keyer speeds up and sending faster
than they’re used to. In some cases the sending improved compared with the lower speeds.
This class meets before breakfast at 6:45am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country
Café.

Bill, W4RK, reported that the intermediate class is also going well with good enthusiasm.
Students are learning to copy in their heads. This class meets after breakfast from 9-10am
at Mel’s.

Kent, K9ZTV, reported that the beginning class has had some new people join in. This
class meets at Mel’s at 10am.
  Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that the Technician class for the Red Cross
  starts Tuesday evening. Ten sets of books will be ordered for 17 students. Since the new
  Technician class study books changed a couple months ago, Mike requested that the club
  consider purchasing a half dozen or so books for use with the training classes. Kent,
  K9ZTV, moved that the club purchase books for the instructors to use. Chuck, AF6GT,
  seconded and the motion passed. Mike will determine the quantity to be ordered.


  Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Laura, KDØGRV, proposed that the club order basic T-
  shirts to get the first order placed with MVE. The shirts will have the small club logo on the
  left chest and a larger logo on the back. Laura will also check on the option of adding a
  callsign as well. The shirts will be colored Oceana Blue. Tom, NØSS, will send out an
  email with instructions for placing orders. The hope is to have them available by the next
  meeting for those who placed orders.


  SEMA Callsign: No report.


  Merit Badge University: Kent, K9ZTV, reported that the six scouts that had signed up to
  work towards the signaling merit badge have successfully completed the requirements and
  will be receiving their badges at the next troop court of honor. A troop in Columbia has
  since contacted Kent and Bill asking if they would help about 20 scouts in their area work
  towards this same goal. On October 30th, Kent, Bill, and Karl will be doing a 3-hour study
  session in Columbia for these scouts and will need more help. Finally, a troop in
  Washington has also requested help for about 20 scouts as well.
