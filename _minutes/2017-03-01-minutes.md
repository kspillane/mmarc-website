New Business:

         1. Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, presented a proposal which was made by the Technical Committee and
            approved by the Board to move the 147.00 repeater from the Holts Summit site to the Capitol
            Region Hospital Campus. The various pros and cons were discussed. The club approved a
            motion that upon approval by the Capitol Region Hospital administration the appropriate steps
            would be taken in accordance with the Technical Committee recommendations to move the
            repeater. The club also approved the expenditure of an appropriate amount of funds to facilitate
            the move including procuring new feedline and installing the repeater equipment/antenna and
            removal of the equipment at the Holts Summit Site. Cost of the project was estimated to be
            between $1000 and $2000.
