  update these lists and to have them available at meetings for those interested.

  ordered and sold for $3 each to interested club members long ago. Tom estimates these
  were from around 15 years ago. Only twelve patches were found and before he knew it, the
  Secretary-Treasurer was inundated with people waving cash eager to purchase these last
  few. Since money talks the meeting was briefly put on hold to pad the bank balance a bit
  more.

Announcements:

  Karl, WA2KBZ, announced that he has achieved the VHF/UHF Century Club (VUCC)
  award. This is award is given to hams who work at least 100 grid squares on VHF and/or
  UHF bands. In addition, Karl has indicated that he has a 6M Moxon antenna that he would
  gladly donate to a new ham who may be interested in getting started with 6 Meter VHF.
  Anyone interested please get in touch with him or Mike, WAØZOG.

  Radio: The Rules, The Risks, The Issues” to those present. This is a very important read
  that everyone should take the time to study, perhaps a couple times. At issue is the notion
  of amateur radio operators working with agencies and organizations and what is permitted
