                               Minutes of the
                       Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.

Meeting called to order by Kurt Bleich (KBØHNR)

the alley on the east side of the church (midway up the alley).

Minutes:  Minutes from the meetings of  July 12, 2001 were read,
corrections noted, motioned and seconded for approval.

2 Meter Repeater Report:   Mid-State Waste interference seems to be
    resolved...  Haven't been picking up other significant problems with
    retransmitting the JCPD, JCFD, Capitol Police.  Someone might be doing
    this on purpose, or FM scanner may be picking up and retransmitting.
    Amp is running 50 - 60 Watts output.  The amp gets so hot that it melts
    solder joints and causes poor conductivity.  Need to find a better way
    of cooling the transmitter with a heat sink or better fan.


Digi-peater Report:  Nothing new to report.

Emergency Coordinator Report:  No activity to cause mobilization.  No Red
    Cross contact made.


Field Day Report:  We need pictures of the activities for the Web Page.


10 Meter CW Net Report:  28.258 MHz every night with emphasis on Wednesday
night.


2 Meter Net:  Updated script for Net Control on the Web Page
Jamboree on the Air:  Having trouble getting in touch with the district
manager of the Boy Scouts.


Halloween Special Event:  WOØO is back in the area.  We might borrow his
    call.  His farm is in Frankenstein.  The last time the club did this
    event we worked a Mr. Frankenstein from Romania that looked for us at
    the scheduled time and frequency.  We might also want to get WØO for a
    call sign. Oct 31st is a Wednesday.  That would be the night of the
    event.

New business:

2 Meter Net Controllers:

Service Dogs:  Would the MMARC be willing to host a talk by Kathy Kermin
    about service dogs that help individuals with illnesses.  The dogs make
    the person aware that they are having a problem and make them take the
    necessary action to regain control.  We may schedule her for a talk in
    September or October.

MS 150:  Ben (NØOFD) is co-ordinating the club's activities in the MS 150
    again this year.  He has 28 volunteers so far, but could use more.
    There was a slight change in the route.  Need more volunteers for the
    sag stops.  We are in good shape for the event. Race starts Saturday
    morning Sep 8th, and concludes Sunday Sep 9th.  Route is reversed on
    second day. 2000 riders expected this year.  Provides training in Net
    Operations.
