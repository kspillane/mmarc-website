Old Business

         Miscellaneous Items:
            1.     This is the 100th anniversary year for ARRL. Each state has two weeks during the year to
                    operate W1AW/Ø. Mid-MO ARC is the club sponsor for the first week (5/28 - 6/3) for
                    Missouri. And we won't need to fill a schedule for 24X7 that week, just the times of good
                    propagation. Karl Schulte, WA2KBZ, had previously agreed to coordinate the operation
                    but has scheduling conflicts; Mitch Odneal, NW0M, will coordinate. A couple of other
                    clubs want to participate. After we determine the number of operators involved, we’ll try
                    to get a schedule set up.
               2.   Kent Trimble, K9ZTV reporting: We have committed to assisting the Boy Scouts in an
                    amateur radio merit badge effort. Bill Gerth, W4RK, and I are still working on this. The
                    event will be in March.
               3.   Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ reported that he is still waiting to hear from State Fair officials
                    about setting up an amateur radio display at the fair.

New Business:

         1. Thanks to John Graves, KB0NXK, for the work he is doing on the website. Also, thanks to Carol
            Abbott, KD0YBL, for taking on the task of keeping the MMARC website calendar updated.
         2. Brief comments regarding Winterfest were solicited – several members reported a few good
            buys.
         3. There have been a lot of questions concerning the amount of money we have in the bank and
            what will be done with it. The Board has discussed having a budget so we can be good stewards
            of that money. The cost of running the club in 2013 (electric & phone bills, and Field Day) was
            $806.00 (rounded). 25 members paid dues in 2013 which amounted to $612.50 so, we were
            $193.50 in the red for 2013. It takes a minimum of 32 dues paying members to operate the club;
            there are 72 members on the sign in sheet so, hopefully, with the change in dues collection and e-
            mail notification, this will remedy itself.
         4. There was discussion regarding our money and budget. One item discussed was gifting money to
            outside organizations. Since we are not a money making organization, our dues are the only
            means we have of support. When the estate gift money is gone, there is no way to replenish it.
            Items discussed were: a) Can we invest the money? Jerry Sill, NN0JS, advised that it is not a
            good idea to do this at this time for a number of reasons, including having to pay taxes on
            investment income and the need for the money to be available for expenditures that may be
            recommend in the future. b) Could we apply for Federal grants? No, our current club status
            precludes this. Suggestions: for outside “gifting”, we could pass the hat. Bequeathed money
            should stay for club use. Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ, made a motion that we make no gifts from club
            funds; there were numerous seconds, and the motion carried.
         5. President Mike Dolson, N0ZH, will present a budget for vote sometime before June.
         6. A request for gifting $45.00 for a Missouri QSO Party Contest plaque. A hat was passed and
            $82.00 was collected.
         7. Bill Gerth, W4RK, has an acquaintance who has a working UHF repeater for sale for $800.00.
            Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, advised it would take additional money for necessary “add-ons”. Tony
            DeWitt, AD0DQ, offered $200.00 toward the purchase. Leonard Neff, KD0YLW, made a motion
            that we make the purchase; second by Steve Hick, WW0G; carried by hand vote.
