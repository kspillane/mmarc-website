2-Meter Repeater report: Jim, NØTKN, reported that a site visit was made for inspection. A
messenger cable was found broken and repaired. The squelch is still being investigated. A
battery for the repeater was discussed. Mike, NØZH, moved that a battery system be
purchased to keep the repeater up and running. Richard, ACØSA, seconded and the motion
passed.


Digipeater report: Rick, KCØPET, passed around a handout detailing the work that had
been performed to bring the digipeater back online. The digipeater is now back to its
permanent home at the Capital Region Medical Center – Stadium Campus.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that a visit was
made to the Americus site. There was high SWR found on the 6M link and it is being
investigated. In spite of this, the system is still working.


Local Emergency Coordinator's report: No report.


District Emergency Coordinator’s report: Joe, KBØQHZ, noted that some folks may need
to have their badges upgraded to the newer Cole County ARES badges. Send an email to
Joe (kb0qhz@arrl.net) if you need an updated badge.


VE Liaison’s report: Nancie, NMØC, reported that after the last big exam session there
were 13 new Technician licensees and 2 upgrades to General. On January 14th there will be
a VE training session for those interested in becoming a Volunteer Examiner. This will be
held at 10am at the JCPD training room.


CW Classes: Kent, K9ZTV, reported that they are focusing on two classes recently. The
advanced class has been copying 3 and 4 letter words at 30wpm and is progressing nicely.
This class meets at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings. The beginning
class has been combined with the intermediate class and things have been going well. This
class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am.


Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that there may be an upgrade class on the
horizon later in the spring or fall.


Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: No report.


Mid-Mo Emergency Radio Service: Jerry, KDØNPF, reported that the incorporators need
to get together to finalize the rough draft of the By-Laws. Due to everyone’s schedules, this
remains an open item.
