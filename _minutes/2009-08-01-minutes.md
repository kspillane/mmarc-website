2-Meter Repeater report: No changes. New modems have been acquired, but nothing
has been done yet.


Digipeater report: Nothing new to report. May want to make the MEPN machine our local
repeater. We need to bring power supply and TNC back to Jack to clean and send back
out.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that two field trips
were made to the site for testing. The equipment is functioning well, but the TNC is the
problem. The system is not identifying the Centralia node. The problem is being worked on
and a solution may be close at hand.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ reported that it was an eventful month.
There was participation in the MO Hospital Association communication exercise at DSR. It
was a coordinated effort between HF receiving and transmitting simplex to the DSR. There
was great cooperation among all participants. Several sites accessed the Show-Me Intertie
to further prove the communications capabilities of the system.

Doug Tice, KDØDAM, has requested help in getting books ordered for the EMCOMM
classes. Tom, NØSS volunteered to help out. Study dates are not yet set. Joe will work
                                                                                     th
with Doug on the dates for both study sessions and test sessions. Currently October 8 is

VE Liaison’s report: Jack, WBØS, reported there was one test that Tom took care of one

will be one to test tonight.


CW Class: Kent, K9ZTV, reports that the focus is on QSO’s in the slow speed class. The
class will then be moving to actual QSO’s with one another. This class meets after
breakfast at 9am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café.

Tom, NØSS, reports that the advanced class is still going well. This class meets before
breakfast at Mel’s at 6:45am.
