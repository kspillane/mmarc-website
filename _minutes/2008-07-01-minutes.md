  Mel’s Country Café.

  Easter Seals Walk/Run: Volunteers need to support event September 20th. The event
  starts around 7a.m. and will end around 11a.m.

  to celebrate the 150th year anniversary of the Central United Church of Christ. Plans are to
  run the station September 13-14. Club to donate $150 to the church.
