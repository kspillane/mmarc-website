Bike MS Wrap Up:
   Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Our club was well represented (12 members) and we got a
   “Thank You” e-mail from the organizers. MS Representative Randy Adler said that no other
   event has the professionalism that the mid-Missouri ride has. There were about 1900 riders on
   Saturday. RFID timing system was used for the ride. There were eleven incidents and nine
   ambulance calls on Saturday. Somebody spread tacks on the route on Saturday. Sunday’s start
   was delayed an hour due to weather and there were 900 riders. The event is on target for two
   million dollars in donations.

SOTA:
  Bill Gerth, W4RK: There was the annual North American SOTA event last weekend. “My wife
  and I went to Taum Sauk Mountain on Saturday. It was very hot and humid. I worked six
  summits plus 40 other stations including 3 European DX contacts, all on five watts. We went to
  Webster County on Sunday where we activated the Webster County High Point and worked 50
  stations. Ohio and Alabama just joined SOTA, which was started in England about ten years
  ago.”
