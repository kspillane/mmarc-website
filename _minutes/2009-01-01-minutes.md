  2-meter Repeater report: No Report

  Digipeater report: KCØCZI went to the site and pulled the transmitter. It was found to be
  dead. It was taken to NØSS for diagnosis. Opening the unit revealed major damage to a
  surface-mount device. Consequently there is no 2M packet transmitter for the digipeater.
  The TNC is presumed to still be good but will not know for sure until the digipeater is put
  back on the air. If anyone has a 2M-monoband radio capable of 25-50 watts output that
  they would consider donating, the digipeater could most likely be repaired and back on the
  air.
  MEPN: Waiting for Channel 2 to shut down analog signal in February, which will help with
  interference problems.

  Emergency Coordinator's report: KBØQHZ sent out a “State of the Union” email from the
  MO section to ARES members concerning plans for the next 3 years. A lot of training is
  being done in the KC area but it is still not clear if that training will spread to STL and JC.
  Level 1 training for this area is being investigated.

  ARES positions are almost filled and a final list will be emailed to ARES members and also
  provided at the next meeting.

  A meeting at St. Mary’s Hospital is planned for January 9th to discuss our participation in any
  responses with the hospital. Also to be discussed is a maintenance plan for the handheld
  radios, batteries, and mobile rigs in addition to a general checkout of the main system on a
  regular basis. Once this is completed, a similar agenda will be scheduled for Capital Region
  Hospital.

  CW Class: Advanced and beginning CW classes continue to meet every Saturday morning
  at Mel’s Country Café. The advanced class is doing a lot of exercises from the last year and
  students are copying in the range of 20-30wpm. The beginning class now knows the
  alphabet with numbers and punctuation being introduced recently.

  DHSS Class: Technician classes will start January 14th from 6pm to 9pm and continue for
  about 6 weeks. Club volunteers would be appreciated for assisting with questions from
  students, not necessarily to teach the material due to the abbreviated course schedule.

  APRS trackers: WNØX has the parts to upgrade the club tracker and is proceeding.

  Storm Spotting Class: Cole County class is scheduled for January 20th in the JCPD
  Training Room. Osage County will have a class in Linn March 5th. It would be good to have
  our members attend one of these courses each year to keep refreshed.

  VE session: A special VE session to be offered Dec 21st 2PM at the Community of Christ
  Church.

New business

  General Class: KMØS would like to request the club to sponsor or otherwise support a
  class to help folks interested in upgrading. A proposed timeframe for this class would be
  late March or early April. Interest in the class was well received and more discussion will
  follow.

  Skywarn Net: KMØS reminded everyone about this net, which meets every Tuesday night
  at 8pm on the Skywarn repeater (146.865 (-), PL Tone 127.3). More activity would be
  appreciated both by check-in stations and net control stations. A roster will be put together
  for running the net.

  KBØQHZ noted that the National Weather Service in St. Louis last year did not hear a lot of
  activity on the 440 MHz link when calling for observations from this area. When bad
weather is near, it would be a good idea to monitor the Show-Me Intertie (443.175 (+), PL
Tone 77).

Suggestions for Upcoming Year: WAØZOG called for ideas for programs to be put on
during the year. Program topics, presenters, club activities, etc. were all welcomed. Some
of the suggestions included:

    Tutorial on RTTY operation

    Presentation on APRS

    Tutorial on Traffic Handling

    Presentation on Contesting

    Presentation on Making PC Boards

    Presentation on HF Antenna Fundamentals

    Presentation on RFI location and remedies

Mayor’s Run for Reading: Event will be held on Saturday, April 18th from 8am to 10am.
Volunteers are requested for providing communications support for the event.

Raffle: WBØAQV raffled off several door prizes to those present.

SKN Presentation: W4RK presented a slide show of Straight Key Night, which was hosted
at his home with several members and spouses participating. Color commentary was
presented by K9ZTV.
Respectfully submitted: Mark Eberle (KIØY), Secretary-Treasurer.
