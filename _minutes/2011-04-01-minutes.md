2-Meter Repeater report: Tom, NØSS, reported that the repeater is still working ok. A site
trip has not yet been made, but the power output needs to be checked. If anyone has a
9600 baud (or higher) external modem that they would be willing to donate, please contact
Tom.


Digipeater report: Rich, NØJBF, reported that he, Rick, Joe, and Tom went and checked
the 9913 coax and found it to be good. It was tied to the tower to secure the run. The
digipeater is up and running at Rick’s house for the time being. Remaining work will be to
install ground rods and install the digipeater back in the shed. Karl donated some large size
ground wire to the cause.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported no changes.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Rich, NØJBF, reported that plans are still moving along
on the National Level Exercise in May. Some folks not going to Dayton have offered
assistance with being on the air. Tentative plans are to be on the air 3-4 hours on Monday,
                                                               th
Tuesday, and Wednesday. The dates are May 16, 17, and 18 .

Kent will be headed to Branson on Monday, operating on Tuesday with the Missouri Funeral
Directors Association’s disaster mortuary drill in conjunction with the NLE. Amateur radio
will be implemented to backup the satellite phones.


VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported that there were two exams last month resulting
in one upgrade and one new licensee. Nancie, NMØC, will be taking over as the new VE
Liaison.


CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reported that the advanced class is still going although turnout
was low last week. Mac’s buddy Evan showed up and tried his hand near the end of the
last class. This class meets at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings.

Kent, K9ZTV, reported that the intermediate class has been having a large turnout and
filling their small room to capacity. The intermediate class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on
Saturday mornings at 9am, while the beginning class meets an hour later at 10am.


Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that the upgrade class for the General license
exam is underway and there are a dozen students participating.


Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: No report.


Red Cross MOU: Rich, NØJBF, reported that Mike Dolson, Andy Adams, and himself met
with Mike Stelzleni to discuss this and decided to follow the outline of the MOU included as
a sample with the ARRL and the National Red Cross. There are 12 counties covered by the
local Red Cross. Andy donated a 5-band vertical antenna and there is still a need for some
donated coax. A presentation will follow later at a time to be determined.
  Grant Money: Mike, WAØZOG, reported that he had not heard back from the Callaway
  Amateur Relay League (CARL). We will wait until we hear from them before deciding to
  how to proceed.


  Field Day: Rich, NØJBF, reported that he needs volunteers for station captains for the
  VHF, GOTA, SSB, and CW stations.

  The previous discussion of using a digital station and alternating with it with the GOTA
  station will not work according to the Field Day rules if we wish to operate in Category 2A.
  To operate a digital station, either the SSB or CW station will need to shut down else we will
  be bumped to the 3A category.

  The CCERT crew/vehicle status is currently unknown.

  Kent, K9ZTV, recommended that we do something to recognize Porite for their support in
  letting us use their field each year. Ideas should be brought to the next meeting.

  As a reminder, to operate at Field Day, your dues need to be paid up.


New business

event will be held on April 23 and will last until approximately 10AM. Six volunteers are
needed to participate from 7:45AM until the event is over providing communication support.
