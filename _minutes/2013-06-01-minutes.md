New Business:
         Kent Trimble, K9ZTV: I got a call from Bill Hudson – the Linn repeater (145.390, 127.3, negative) is
         being activated on Skywarn, which did not get a lot of use during the last storm. Weather service is
          very interested in hail size. Linn net is Tuesday at 8:30 p.m., Skywarn is same night at 8:00 p.m.
          Roger Conway, KD0FAS, has been diagnosed with cancer and it is fairly advanced. (Roger passed
          away June 25th.) Ken Barnes came by breakfast a few weeks ago and asked for CW operators to
          assist with Scouts in an annual event. Several members assisted and about 180 Cubs participated.

Announcements:
        Mitch Odneal, NW0M, reporting on the Dayton Hamvention experience. He went over a day early
        and got to meet a lot of contesters at the hotel. Mitch went to “Contest University” on Thursday –
        there were different sessions all day long about many aspects of operating, not just contesting.
        Actual Hamvention started on Friday with the swapmeet starting an hour early at Hare Arena. Luso
        Tower Company had an exhibit – a base seven feet across requiring 52 yards of concrete. The tower
        weighs 50 tons and costs $56,000.00 plus shipping.
