  Current Bank Balance: $7,252.07

Old business


  2-Meter net report: Mike Dolson, NØZH Net Manager. There have been four full months of
  reporting since Mike filled the role of Net Manager. For the month of November there were
  a total of 86 check-ins over four sessions. Thanks to all who volunteer to call the nets.
  More folks are encouraged to volunteer as Net Control so we aren’t always relying on the
  same individuals.

  2-Meter Repeater report: Tim, KAØOUV, and Tom, NØSS went out and did some
  cleaning at the repeater. Tim inadvertently reset the squelch so Tom went back to fix. The
  autopatch is back up and running at this time. The burbling and loss of sensitivity problems
  remain.

  The autopatch is available to all current members of the club. Speed dial options are
  available. If interested in more information, contact Tom.


  Digipeater report: No report.


  Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that the system is
  currently on the air and working well. There are still some connection problems getting into
  St. Louis probably due to the high noise floor. There is interest in moving the system out
  west with a possible node in Macon.

  Currently, a sponsor is being sought for the Callaway node. The Callaway Amateur Radio
  League in Fulton is considering sponsorship.


  Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ reported that things have been quiet
  with little activity. There is still no news on the new requirements for the EMCOMM
  instructors.


  VE Liaison’s report: No report. Please remember to give some notice if you wish to test
  after the monthly meetings.


  CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reports that the advanced class is working deeper on traffic
  handling. There are simulations of calling a CW net and passing traffic. Tom has made a
  set of handouts for all members of the three classes. This class meets before breakfast at
  6:45am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café.

  Kent, K9ZTV, reports that the intermediate class is going well. This class meets after
  breakfast from 9-10am at Mel’s.

  The new beginning class has two to four students. They are currently working on numbers.
  This class meets at 10am after breakfast and the slow speed class.
  Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported there are 8 people who are confirmed to take
  the Extra upgrade course. Training materials will be targeted to be distributed to the
  students by the end of the year. There are also between 8-16 people interested in a
  Technician class to be held later in the spring.

  The Extra class will be roughly 8 to 10 weeks meeting on Thursday nights with the
  exception of club meeting nights. The time will be from 6:30 or 7:00pm and will last from
  two to two and a half hours. The price for training materials is $35. Please confirm to Mike
  via email at km0s@arrl.net if you are interested in taking the class.


  Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: No update.


  Officer Nominations: Nominations for club officers were as follows:

  President: Mike Miller, WAØZOG
  Vice-President: Rick Bennett, KCØPET
  Secretary-Treasurer: Mark Eberle, KIØY
  Board Member: Nancie McAnaugh, KDØDDY

  Tom, NØSS, moved that the slate of nominees be accepted by acclamation. The motion
  passed unanimously.


New business

  None


Announcements:

  Larry, AKØLW, mentioned that the club’s website could use some updating. The website is
  updated when the webmaster is notified of changes that need to be made. Rick
  volunteered to discuss the issue with the officers and board members about what can be
  done to improve the site.

  Larry, AKØLW, also noted that the weekly net preamble is quite lengthy and wonders if it
  should be shortened. A short discussion followed regarding various net styles. It was
  suggested that comments on how the net is handled including any revisions to the preamble
  be forwarded to Mike, NØZH. More discussion will follow once the comments are gathered.

  Karl, WA2KBZ, suggested that anyone interested in the Mirage 100W amplifier for 432Mhz
  to look for the version with the blue buttons which were made in California rather than the
  later versions made by MFJ which aren’t of as good quality.

  Dave West, WBØATD, has donated some late model QST magazines for those interested.

  Jack, NØKSF, stated that if anyone is in need of meters for projects to contact him. He
  currently has over one hundred in his possession and would gladly share.
