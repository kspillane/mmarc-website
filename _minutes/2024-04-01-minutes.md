                                      Minutes of the
                              Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.
                                    Jefferson City, MO

Meeting of: April 11, 2024

Presentation:

   •   Ozarkcon:

       Club members Bill- W4RK, Kent- K9ZTV, Jack- N0KSF, Bryan- K0EMT, Rich-
       N0EAX attended Ozarkcon. Bryan organized and led a group of POTA activators near
       Table Rock Lake Dam in activations just prior to the beginning of the conference. He
       later presented a conference talk that included the results of their activations. Jack talked
       about the T41 kit project that he is working on and his surprise at the size of a completed
       T41 that was on display at the conference. Jack and Kent enjoyed the presentations on the
       Pirate, Spy, and Candestine Radio stations and on the history of the Hallicrafter’s
       receivers. Kent mentioned the presentation on the new Four State QRP (4SQRP) kits
       being designed by David Cripes- NM0S. Kent showed the new 4SQRP paddle that he
       had purchased and had just built. Jack built the same paddle at the Conference Build-A-
       Thon.

   •   Field Day:

       Kent contacted Porite concerning the use of the Field Day site. The site is no longer
       available to MMARC as the site has been sold to the State of Missouri and they are not
       interested in use by MMARC. Other sites were discussed but nothing resolved. Kent
       recommended that any new locations should include bathroom facilities and air
       conditioning. Vehicles were discussed in some detail but would be needed based on the
       site chosen. Tim will inquire on the use of one of the Highway Patrol vehicles and the
       tower trailer. Tim will also find out what State organization will be involved with the
       Porite site. He will confirm their lack of interest with MMARC.

       Kent received a letter from our insurance provider indicating they will no longer cover
       our club policy. Kent has and will continue to try to contact a new carrier recommended
       by the ARRL. Kent has been providing the club a one year liability policy ($325 most
       recently). The current policy runs to June 18, 2024.
   •    Missouri QSO Party:

        Tim gave a summary of the club involvement with the Missouri QSO Party (MQP)
        operation. Tim and Jim- N0LBY operated from Jim’s Osage County station and initial
        results placed them in third place in their category with 604 contacts. Jim operated on
        phone with Tim on CW. Tim noted a problem with software that was resolved after
        several hours. There was some digital operation with Tim noting a configuration issue
        while logging in FT4 and FT8. This issue will be reviewed by the MQP organizers as it
        has been resolved in another state. Mitch- NW0M operated in the MQP, and initial
        results placed him first in his CW, low power, assisted category. Craig- N0JN operated
        also and indicated 10 or 15 contacts involving the MQP. Art- KD0GY enjoyed the event
        with a couple hundred contacts.
