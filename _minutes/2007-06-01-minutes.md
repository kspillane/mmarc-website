                                     Minutes of the
                             Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.
                                   Jefferson City, MO

Meeting of: June 14, 2007

Called to order by: President Kent Trimble (K9ZTV)

In attendance: K9ZTV, WØESE, NØSS, KØJNW, KC7ARR, KCØPET, WBØS, KBØNXX,
KCØLZG, WØIFL, WAØZOG, KDØAKF, NØZH, NØEAX, KCØSJR, NØROK, KØVWM,
WBØTPN, KCØZLB, KCØJDW, KCØCZI, WDØFDU, WBØKXP, KBØNXK, Tom Shea

Minutes (presented & approved, unless otherwise stated):

Bank balance: $ 7, 350.86

Old business:

  2-meter net report:
                                 May 16        KØJNW        8
                                 May 23        NØKSF        13
                                 May 30        KCØUZA       8
                                 June 6        NØSS         13
                                 June 13       KØJNW        6


  2-meter net controllers:
                                 June 20       WØESE        12
                                 June 27       NØSS         14
                                 July 4        KØJNW        17
                                 July 11       KCØCZI


  2-meter Repeater report: Repeater is working fine. The weather has delayed any work to
  done.

  Digipeater report: Club discussed little to no activity on the digipeater. Richard Stump
  offered to check it.

  MEPN: No new news.
  Emergency Coordinators report: Richard Stump discussed a 3-day earthquake exercise.
  It will begin June 19th. A few local hams are needed to participate.

  CW Class: The CW class continues to meet every Saturday, at 0645 (before breakfast), at
  Mel’s Country Café.


  New business:

  Club VP resigns and is replaced: Steve, NNØB reigned as club Vice President due to a
  family move to New Mexico. Elections were held to replace him. Mike, WAØZOG, was
  elected as MMARC Vice President, to fill out Steve's remaining term.

  Field Day 2007: The club discussed field day. Club equipment has been organized by
  Tom and Kent. The club is in good shape for dinner. Kevin, KCØJDW, has donated some
  antennas and a small rotator to the club for VHF. The club will be evaluating a mast by the
  Blue Sky Mast Company (on loan from the manufacturer). NØSS has rebuilt the club
  dipoles.

  Easter Seals Walk: KØJNW reported on the walk and thanked those who helped.

  MOHP Tech class: Roger Strope with the Missouri Highway Patrol discussed an upcoming
  Tech class to be held with the Highway Patrol Academy. He asked for help with instructors
  and VE exams. The class is to begin June 26th.

  JC Police ARES station: Doug Keeney, WDØFDU, from the JC Police Department
  reminded the club about the ARES station located at the Police Department. The station
  has not been used much and needs to be used. Doug will conduct training for the station at
  next meeting for those interested in getting access to it.

  Corwin Event QST article: Kent, K9ZTV, has submitted an article about MMARC special
  event W9C. He received a letter from the ARRL asking to allow the article to appear on the
  website as the “Web Extra” article. Kent has written them a letter back asking them to
  reconsider the article for publication in QST.

  VE exams: None

  Program: Jack, WBØS talked about ALC Pictures (Springfield MO), a company that will
  print high-quality QSL cards.

Respectfully submitted: Bryan Braunschweig (WØESE), Secretary-Treasurer.

Visit the MMARC web page at: http://www.mmccs.com/mmarc
