Old Business:

          1. We voted a donation last month to Central United Church of Christ and received a nice letter of
             response from the interim pastor. Letter was read to membership.
          2. At the MS Bike Ride, there was a pile-up on Saturday, but Sunday was very quiet. Next year’s
             ride will be headquartered in Godfrey, Illinois due to the Boone County Fairgrounds closing.
          3. This Saturday, September 13th, is the Zonta 5K at Memorial Park. The event runs from 0800 to
             1000; we need about six members to arrive for communications at about 0730. This is a very
             low key operation and is a good opportunity for new hams to learn what we do. We will work
             this on the K0ETY repeater.
          4. We would like to get the St. Mary’s project completed by the end of September. However, we
             are still waiting for delivery of the antennas, and the UHF repeater system still needs some work
             to finalize settings on the new controller and linking to the VHF repeater.
