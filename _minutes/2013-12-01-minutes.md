New Business:

          1. Newly elected President Mike Dolson resigned his position as 2 meter net manager. Steve Hick
             was nominated and elected to fill the position.
          2. ARRL station W1AW was granted call sign W100AW for the duration of 2014 as this is the
             ARRL’s 100th anniversary year. The call sign will be used during Straight Key Night this New
             Year’s Eve and Day.
          3. Bill Gerth, W4RK, reporting: I am liaison with Capitol Region Medical Center and recently
             attended a Region F planning meeting. All hospitals are required to have periodic disaster
             exercises. Jon Johnson represented St. Mary’s Health Center. Dave Bagge, Joe Betros, and Jerry
             Sill also attended. There was extensive discussion about the needs – the two hospitals will
             determine their specific needs and another meeting will be announced later.
          4. Greg Abbott, WB0AQV, and wife attended the Missouri Disaster Preparedness Conference. Many
             others attended. Greg described various ham-related capabilities to the attendees. He suggested
             we should be prepared so we can leave on a moment’s notice to assist in disasters. General
             recommendation is for seven day’s supply of food and water in homes at all times. Greg stressed
             that there is to be no “self-deployment,” we must wait until we are called or requested.
             Suggestion was made to Greg that he and his wife prepare a presentation for a later MMARC
             meeting.
          5. Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, mentioned that we have been asked to assist with Boy Scouts in March.
             He will have more information at the January meeting.
