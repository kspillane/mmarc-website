VE Liaison’s report: No report.


CW Classes: Kent, K9ZTV, reported that all three CW classes are continuing to meet.
Someone will be needed to take over leading the advanced class. The advanced class meets
at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings. The intermediate class meets at
Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am, while the beginning class meets an hour
later at 10am.


Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that there are no classes scheduled for the fall
at this time. A VE class has been discussed consisting of one session on a Saturday. Also,
a couple classes on operating are being looked into.


Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Bryan, KDØGRU, passed around a sample hat and jacket for
people to look at. Pricing is still pending and when that is finalized, pictures and pricing will
be posted to the club’s website.


New Organization: Mike, WAØZOG, reported that Jerry, KDØNPF, and Rich, NØJBF, have
been working hard to get the paperwork ready for submitting to the Secretary of State to
establish a new 501(c)(3) organization. This will allow us to apply to for grant money that
may become available.
New business

  Repeater Trustee: The club needs to have someone appointed as the new trustee for the
  repeater. Several discussions took place involving what the trustee’s responsibilities include
  as it is more than just serving as a maintenance person. Mike, KMØS, moved that
  individuals interested in serving as the trustee submit their names to the Secretary-Treasurer
  who will then present the names to the Board members for consideration. Bryan, KDØGRU,
  seconded and the motion passed. A two-week timeframe was recommended for submitting
  names.
