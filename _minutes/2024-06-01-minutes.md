Old Business:

   •   The July meeting will include a program on MESH presented by Jason, KF0QIL.

   •   Tim discussed the reason for needing to purchase bandpass filters to protect the radios
       owned by club members. These radios are used by club members in events like Field
       Day and the Frankenstein Special Event Station. The uncertainties associated with
       antenna setup in unknown locations prompted the need to revisit the bandpass filter
       purchase. MMARC Board members had a meeting on this issue and it was decided to
       purchase two filters from Hamation for use during the upcoming Field Day. An internet
       vote of club members took place showing support for the purchase. The filters were
       ordered, received, and were successfully utilized during Field Day.
