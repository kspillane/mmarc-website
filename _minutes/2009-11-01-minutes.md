2-Meter Repeater report: No changes. Tom is still trying to coordinate a time to get the
repeater pulled and do testing along with Tim and/or Dan


Digipeater report: Still not working. Tom and Jack are discussing options.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that he, Tom, and
Bill went out to Americus, MO to install the node on 6M. It is currently on the air and
working well.

An instruction manual is being written to describe how to use the system and will be made
available for those wishing to know more.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ reported that there is some feedback on
the advanced EMCOMM class. The proposed requirements do not make much sense.
There are also considerable perquisites including many FEMA courses. Dennis Gideon has
been asked to look into the situation.

We are set up to test for EMCOMM Level 1. Twenty-one people passed the exam on
          th
October 24 .


VE Liaison’s report: Jack, WBØS, reported that 9 exams were given on November 5th at
the Highway Patrol Test Session resulting in 7 new Technician licensees.

There will be one test tonight after the meeting. Jack has also proposed another VE
session in the near future.


CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reports that the advanced class is still going well. The class is
working on traffic handling. This class meets before breakfast at 6:45am every Saturday
morning at Mel’s Country Café.

Kent, K9ZTV, reports that the class is now known as an intermediate class. This class
meets after breakfast from 9-10am at Mel’s.

A new beginning class has started with two students who are off and running. This class
meets at 10am after breakfast and the slow speed class.


Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that we have been asked to assist with a
Technician class for up to 30 students in the April or May timeframe. Also an Extra class
study session is being looked into starting in December and going through February. A
General class study session will be planned for next fall. Mike will send an email to the
distribution list to find out who is interested in the Extra classes.
  Halloween Special Event Station (WØO): The event was a huge success and even
  managed to land the club on the front page of the Unterrified Democrat, the weekly
  newspaper in Linn, MO.

  There were 300 combined contacts, 130 on SSB and 170 on CW. The log sheet showed
  about 20-25 signatures which made for a great turnout.

  The Warrensburg club reserved WØF and did the event on their own. They were happy
  with the arrangement and agreed that it does help to simplify QSLing.

  Judy’s daughter designed the QSL card for this year’s event and did an outstanding job!

  Ideas for next year include setting up on Saturday and then begin operating on Sunday
  morning around 8am continuing until around 7 or 8pm.
