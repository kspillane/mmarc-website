Announcements:

  KDØDDY requested the help of K9ZTV and W4RK to talk to the sponsoring senator
  regarding legislation (bill SB-30) which prohibits text messaging while driving. As
  introduced, there is no exclusion for amateur radio. Thanks to this effort the bill has been
  amended to correct this and it is now before the Public Safety Committee for review.
  Thanks to Nancie for flagging this item!

  NØSS received a letter from William Woods University regarding the availability of meeting
  rooms. Thanks to this well timed letter, we were able to make use of their facility for the
  April meeting. There is no fee for the first time use, but should we elect to make more
  regular use of the facilities there would be a usage fee to be accounted for.

  KDØFAS presented a form that could be kept in your car for use in helping to report traffic
  accident information. These forms were made available to anyone interested.

  Karl Schulte is doing better but is not back to 100%. Keep him in your prayers as we wish
  him back to good health.
