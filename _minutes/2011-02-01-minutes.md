2-Meter Repeater report: Tom, NØSS, reported that the repeater is functioning well.
There have been no reports of dropouts, sensitivity loss, etc.


Digipeater report: Rick, KCØPET, reported that the progress has been slow in packaging
the digipeater into the enclosure. Steve, KDØGRM, and Nancie, NMØC, donated some
ground rods for use at the site. Installation will wait for warmer weather. There is still a
need for some #2 or larger ground wire.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported the system is
working fine. The 6M antenna favors the east. To connect to Columbia it is best to connect
through Callaway first. The network was used with success during the recent
communications exercise.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ, reported that the communications
                            th
exercise held on January 27 went well and had a good turnout. There was good
participation among local and out of county hams. A lot of comments were received for
future events and some problems were identified with the packet system.

J.D., WAØBER, added that there were two after action items that stood out. There is a
need for more trained message handlers and more NCS operators for the local repeaters.
As a side note, the National Guard is looking to add ham operators for their armories.

Rich, NØJBF, in trying to coordinate the efforts of the Jefferson City ring, thanked all those
that participated in the exercise. There were six stations in the ring which was the largest
turnout yet.

Joe announced that due to a conflict of interest, he has resigned as the Emergency

NØJBF.

  VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported that there were two exams last month and

  CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reported that the advanced class is going well. All are
  improving and Tom is trying to come up with some different methods of teaching. This
  class meets at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings.

  Bill, W4RK, reported the intermediate and beginning classes are also going well. Kent,
  K9ZTV, and Bill will be bringing radios to class to demonstrate how to zero beat CW
  signals. The intermediate class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at
  9am, while the beginning class meets an hour later at 10am.


  Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that there are requests for two more training
  classes. A technician class and a general class will be investigated. Emails have been sent
  to the instructors and the next item to be worked out will be when the classes are held.


  Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Mark, KIØY, reported that there are a limited number of
  patches that were made when the recent T-shirt order was placed. They were made
  available after the meeting for purchase.


  By-Laws: Mark, KIØY, reported that the proposed updates to the by-laws had been sent
  via email to all current members for review. There was a lengthy discussion about the
  changes, but voting will take place at a future meeting. All additional comments to be
  considered should be sent to the club officers directly or through the link on the website.

  Understanding (MOU) from the Red Cross to be agreed upon between the two

  Grant Money: Karl, WA2KBZ, reported that he was approached with an opportunity to
  apply for some grant money for the club. Karl will investigate further and report back.
