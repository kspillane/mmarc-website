VE Liaison’s Report:
Mike Smith - AD0YM

July 2020 meeting:

There were no test sessions offered since our last meeting. Again, the last test session was offered Saturday, 7
March 2020.

I have had a couple of inquiries from folks outside of the club this past month. One direct, someone trying to
find an active VE team in the region and another on behalf of someone currently preparing to test.

Seems like everytime I might consider setting up a "special" test session, current event status suggests
continuing to wait until the club starts meeting in person again.

A search of the ARRL Test Session Website on Tuesday, 7 July, found no test sessions scheduled within 100
miles of Jefferson City through 30 September 2020. Using a different filter, "Missouri", there were some
listings, though the banner across the top includes a disclaimer, "Due to the Coronavirus outbreak, please verify
with your VE team that the exam session is being held." I suspect many of those are previously programmed
listings that have not been updated.

Mike Smith, AD0YM
Mid-MO ARC VE Liaison
