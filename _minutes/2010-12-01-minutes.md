2-Meter Repeater report: Tom, NØSS, reported that the repeater is still working. There
are still power issues to resolve though. We now have 3 backup batteries.


Digipeater report: Rick, KCØPET, reported that the digipeater is still on the test bench.
Rick is assembling it into a new package. It is hoped to be completed by the January
meeting so that Rick can give a presentation on how to use the digipeater.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Tom, NØSS, reported that the network
was down for a week or two due to a lightning hit on the 6M dipole. Jim, NØTKN, went to
the site recently to check on things after some work had been done and found that
everything is functional again. A more extensive grounding system will also be put in.

VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported that there will be a testing session following
the meeting with 5 or 6 taking exams. There have been about 7-8 new ham licensees

CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reported that the advanced class is going well. There will be a
special CW class coming up where the students will use either a straight key or bug. This is
to prepare for the upcoming Straight Key Night on New Year’s Eve. This class meets at
Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings.

Kent, K9ZTV, reported that there were some teenage boys join the beginning CW class
recently. By the end of the class, they had mastered their names and a good portion of the
alphabet. The intermediate class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at
9am, while the beginning class meets an hour later at 10am.


Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported, that the Red Cross class has been
completed. There has been a request to consider having another Technician class this
spring. Prior to this, there were plans to possibly have a General class in the spring for
those just completing the Technician class at the Red Cross. Depending on how many
instructors are available and time constraints, the feasibility of holding both classes will be
looked into.
