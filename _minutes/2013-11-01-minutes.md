          By Laws Change:
             The change to the dues policy making dues payable on June 1st of each year was approved at the
             October meeting. This change created a conflict in Article 7, section 1, paragraph B of the by
             laws. Motion to change this portion of the by laws made by Joe Betros, KB0QHZ, and seconded
             by Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ. Notice will be published by e-mail. Vote on the motion will take
             place at the December meeting.

          Frankenstein Event:
             Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, reporting – eighteen members participated. We had about 300 contacts
             with a near-50/50 split between sideband and CW. The Wednesday evening 2 meter net was run
             from Frankenstein. So far, we have received 65 QSL requests. Thanks to Mike Dolson, N0ZH,
             for maintaining the listing of the event on QRZ.com.

Announcements:
        1) Several members attended the ARRL Midwest Convention held in Lebanon on Friday and
           Saturday, November 8th and 9th. Three members operated W1AW during the event and received
           certificates for their participation. There were 12 minute time slots for each operator – Kent had
           to adapt his paddle to their Icom rig. Dave Sumner, CEO of ARRL did a presentation on the
           upcoming 100th anniversary of ARRL which will be celebrated throughout 2014. Details are in
           the latest issue of QST magazine. There was a lot of vendor support but flea market seemed to
           be lacking. There were also a lot of public service vehicles on site.
        2) Thanks extended to several members for assistance with erecting Rich Kreiser’s tower/antenna
           and Greg Abbott’s tower/antenna.
        3) Bill Gerth, W4RK, extended thanks to all who assisted with the installation at the Sheriff’s Dept.
           and noted that Sheriff Greg White said that we are welcome to use the rig any time.
        4) There will be a meeting on December 12th at Capitol Region Medical Center to discuss plans for
           an all-hospital exercise scheduled for January 8th.
        5) There will be a Straight Key Night starting at 6:00 p.m. on December 31st and running to 11:59
           p.m. on January 1st.
        6) There was a nice article in a recent News Tribune edition about veterans. It included a photo of
           the Willis Corwin memorial.
        7) Several contests are coming up – details will be announced later.

There was no Program.
