Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that there are plans to have Technician and

Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Bryan, KDØGRU, and Laura, KDØGRV, presented the club
with information on using Missouri Vocational Enterprises (MVE) for all the club’s needs in
this area. They have the best prices out of all the options examined. Samples were
presented to the club showing some examples. Patches will be $3.50 each. Jackets and
shirts are available in all sorts of styles, colors, and prices. Embroidery can be added to any
item for less than $2. Bryan and Laura will select a few items for consideration including
polo shirts, jackets, t-shirts, baseball caps, and patch styles. These will then be presented at
a future meeting for further narrowing down.

  Foxhunting: Jon, NØOFJ, gave a short presentation on foxhunting. The hunts are held at
  9:30am on Saturday mornings and last no longer than 3 hours. They tried a foxhunt in the
  Jefferson City area recently with a 5-mile radius to get some participation in this area. The
  next hunt will be held in Ashland and will encompass a 10-mile radius. Contact Jon if
  interested in more information on any of the upcoming hunts.

  Merit Badge University: Kent, K9ZTV, reported that there were 350 scouts that attended
  the university held at Linn State Technical College. Six of these scouts signed up for the
  Signaling Badge. Bill, W4RK, and Steve, KDØGRM, put together kits for Morse code
  sounders that the scouts could build. The scouts will meet with Kent, Bill, and Karl over the
  next two weeks to work on the skills needed to complete the merit badge.

  The out of pocket expense to put the kits together totaled $110.88. A motion was made to
  reimburse Bill for this expense and passed.
