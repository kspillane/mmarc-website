 2 Meter Repeater Report:  Discussion of the interference from local public
    service agencies.  KAØOUV is suspecting that it may be a mixing problem
      There are numerous other VHF and UHF signals on the same tower owned
    by Tri-Star Communications and this tower is only 3 miles from the
    repeater tower.
    Members in the Holts Summit area should listen for the spur signal on
    simplex to see how strong the signal is.  Dealing with two separate
    problems… one is a mixing problem, the other may be someone re-
    broadcasting them.

Digi-peater Report:

Emergency Coordinator Report:


Roster of Members available for shared use?  Steve (KCØACQ) discussed the
    possibility of creating a roster of members that may include addresses,
    e-mail addresses, station equipment, etc.  Privacy issues seem to be
    limited to not putting it on the Internet.  Having the information
    available would help us share information with each other.  This is
    something that we will start putting together over the next several
    months.  Most of the information can be gathered by e-mail and the list
    distributed by e-mail as well.
Jamboree on the Air:  Ron, Kurt and Bryan worked Boy Scout activity from
    the basement of St. Peters church.  It made a good impression and
    practice for the JOTA.  October 20th and Laurie, MO.  JOTA, Send-a-
    Message in CW, and Fox Hunt station will all be part of the activities.
     Call for operators needed!  Events are from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
    Hundreds of scouts are expected to attend and these are very active
    events.  AC will be available.  K9ZTV (Kent) would like to recommend
    that the club take this event very seriously and make a real effort to
    provide a good introduction to the scouts every year.

Halloween Special Event:  It was decided that we did not have enough
    interest or sufficient preparation to make this event a success, and
    therefore, shelved the activity until next year when we can appoint a
    coordinator and do it right.


New business:

The MMARC members present agreed to transfer the old repeater cabinet to
KBØHNR in appreciation of his dedication and service to the amateur
community.

K-2 Transceiver Demonstration will be given as a ‘show and tell’ after next
month’s meeting by KCØOUV (Tim) and KCØIFL (Ron).
