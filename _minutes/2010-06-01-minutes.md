class meets before breakfast at 6:45am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café.

Field Day exchanges. The next goal was to work on logging with the N1MM log program.
The advanced class meets after breakfast from 9-10am at Mel’s while the beginning class
meets at 10am.
