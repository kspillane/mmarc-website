2-Meter Repeater report: Tom, NØSS reported the autopatch is back up. Power was lost
at the site one evening and came back the next day. Tom reset the UPS which was alerting
after visiting the site.


Digipeater report: No report.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that the system is
still on the air and working well. The SEMA packet station is now up and running.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ reported that there have been no major
happenings. There is a hospital exercise scheduled for Friday, the 15th.


VE Liaison’s report: Jack, WBØS, reported that one exam was given recently but the
individual missed one too many questions to pass. After the meeting tonight there will be 3
exams given.


CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reports that the advanced class has added a couple new
members and is putting more time into sending and receiving rather than visiting This class
meets before breakfast at 6:45am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café.

Bill, W4RK, reports that the intermediate class is working on contest exchanges, including
complex exchanges like in the ARRL November Sweepstakes. This class meets after
breakfast from 9-10am at Mel’s.

Kent, K9ZTV, added that the new beginning class is still available for those who know little
to nothing and would like to learn. This class meets at 10am after breakfast and the slow
speed class.


Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported the Extra study group will begin next
Thursday at the Department of Health building at 920 Wildwood in Jefferson City. The
training materials are to be received next week, but more will need to be ordered due to
additional people interested. The Extra class will be held from 6:30 to 8:30 (possibly till 9:00
some nights) every Thursday evening except for club meeting nights.

Plans for a Technician class are being investigated for later in the Spring possibly to be held
on Saturdays. More information will be available later.

Mike, WAØZOG, added that the ARRL study guides for all three license classes are
available at Amazon.com in electronic format (Kindle, I-pod touch, etc.). The price is $9.99.


Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Mike, WAØZOG, has tried contacting the person he
originally spoke with about these but has not had any luck recently. He will continue to get
in touch with her, but in the meantime, if anyone else knows of other contacts, please let
Mike know.
New business

  SEMA Callsign: Jim, NØTKN, reminded everyone that the SEMA packet station still does
  not have a callsign. The FCC rules prohibit an employee from using his/her callsign for this
  purpose. Jim asked the club to sponsor the callsign. Discussion about the club’s call or
  getting a new club call took place. Jim will chair a committee to look into solutions.

  Programs for the year: Mike, WAØZOG, has asked for suggestions on programs for the
  year to be put on after each meeting. Suggestions include (in no particular order):

    Presentation on MEPN by Roger Volk, KØGOB to be done at the February meeting.

    An Old Time Radio Night, by Karl, WA2KBZ

    Radon presentation, by Greg, WBØAQV and Nancie, KDØDDY

    History of Radio/Telegraphy, by Rick, KCØPET

    QRP Operating

    Lightning & Grounding, by Karl, WA2KBZ

    Traffic Handling, by Kent, K9ZTV

    PC Boards, by Tom, NØSS

  some of the larger hamfests regarding proper traffic handling.
