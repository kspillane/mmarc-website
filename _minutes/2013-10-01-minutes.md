Cole Co. Sheriff’s Dept Installation:
   Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: Equipment was installed over two Tuesdays. We purchased
   a 40 foot capable mast, extended it to 28 feet at secured it with guy lines. Thanks to all those
   who helped. Following equipment was installed: dual band antenna, HF dipole Windham, a
   MESH node, V71 dual band radio, 30 amp power supply with Power Poles ready for carry-in HF
   rig. The system is working well – it is able to hit most repeaters in the area. Lightning arrestors
   were also installed. We spent about $2,200.00 and had a few donated items. Joe Betros,
   KB0QHZ, suggested that the Wednesday night net be conducted from the Sheriff’s Dept. once it
   is fully operational.

By Laws Change:
   A change to the dues policy making dues payable on June 1st of each year was proposed at the
   September meeting. During discussion, it was suggested that members get a “bye” until the June
   1st following their current due date rather than pro-rating dues. The affected part of the by laws is
   article 6, section 3. After discussion, the proposal passed unanimously. It was mentioned that
   this change will impact other sections of the by laws and require changes; there are numerous
   sections which list specifics dates, all of which will need to be changed. This will be voted on at
   the November meeting.
