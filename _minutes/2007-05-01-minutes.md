                                     Minutes of the
                             Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.
                                   Jefferson City, MO

Called to order by: President Kent Trimble (K9ZTV)

In attendance: K9ZTV, NNØB, WØESE, NØSS, W4RK, KØJNW, NØKSF, KC7ARR,

Minutes (presented & approved, unless otherwise stated):

  2-meter net report:
                                 April 18     W4RK       8
                                 April 25     KCØPET     15
                                 May 2        KCØLZG     12

                                 May 16       KØJNW      8

                                 June 6       NØSS       13

  2-meter Repeater report: The noise issue reported last meeting has gone away. NØSS
  and KAØOUV to investigate.

  Digipeater report: No problems noted.

  MEPN: No new news.

  Emergency Coordinators report: Local hams are standing by in case of activation for the
  flood of 2007. SEMA has been activated. The club discussed the flood of 1993 and
  MMARC involvement was described.
  CW Class: The CW class continues to meet every Saturday, at 0645 (before breakfast), at
  Mel’s Country Café.

  License class: NØSS reported that the class has now ended and that three (3) new
  Technician Class licensees resulted. Three more are waiting to take their exams. The club
  thanked Tom for his work teaching the class.


  New business:

  Field Day 2007: Station captains were appointed as follows: GOTA, Rich NØEAX; VHF,
  Kevin Wren KCØJDW, SSB, Mike Miller WAØZOG, CW Bryan Braunschweig WØESE.

  Easter Seals Walk: KØJNW reported that he has 4 hams to help. One person to run a
  water station at the half waypoint.

  WØS: Kent, K9ZTV and Steve, NNØB reported on the WØS special event station held in
  Branson April 14-15. Both Kent and Steve were able to operate. They reported that the
  weather was very cold but they enjoyed participating. The event may occur again next
  year.

  Linn Repeater: The 2-Meter Linn repeater is on the air! 145.390, PL 127.3

  BS7H: NØSS discussed the recent DXPedition to Scarborough Reef.

  VE exams: Cris Kallenbach (KCØVRZ), Eugene MO and John Anderson (KCØNFY),
  Chamois, MO both passed the General exam and upgraded from technician.

  Program: Andrey, W3AMB gave a Power Point presentation about some DXPeditions he
  participated in while in Russia.

Respectfully submitted: Bryan Braunschweig (WØESE), Secretary-Treasurer.
