Old Business

           1. Boy Scout Merit Badge Project: Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, and Bill Gerth, W4RK, coordinating;
              Steve Hick, WW0G, Andy Adams, WN0I, and Rich Glassner, N0EAX, will assist. The event will
              be this Saturday at Thomas Jefferson Middle School. Plan is to start with lecture format, then an
              operation portion. We’ll probably use the General portion of the 20 and 40 meter bands.
           2. Thanks to John Graves, KB0NXK, and Carol Abbott, KD0YBL, who continue to update the
              website.
           3. The Board is working on a club budget – it will be presented at the May meeting.
           4. Last meeting, the club voted on a policy to not give club money to other organizations. President
              Mike Dolson, N0ZH, suggested amending this policy to exclude the church which does not
              charge us to use the room. After discussion, the majority of those present agreed that donations
              to the church were acceptable, but they should be made at random intervals rather than an annual
              donation.
           5. Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ, reported that he is still awaiting a response from State Fair officials
              regarding a Ham Radio display at the Fair. He added that he sparked an interest in amateur radio
              with an employee of a Columbia bike shop.
           6. The funding of a linked UHF repeater was approved at our February meeting. Tony DeWitt,
              AD0DQ, graciously donated $200.00 toward the purchase.
           7. Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, made a presentation on the cost of three projects: Needed work on the
              current repeater site, the K0ETY backup site, and the UHF site at the new St. Mary’s Health
              Center. These projects had been discussed at the Board meeting and approved for forwarding to
              the general membership. Total cost for the three projects is $9,552.00. Kent Trimble, K9ZTV
              made the motion to approve the expenditure and was seconded by Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ. By a
              show of hands the motion carried unanimously.
           8. At the February meeting, a collection was taken up to support sponsoring of a plaque for the
              Missouri QSO Party which will be the first weekend in April. Enough donations were collected
              for us to sponsor two plaques – the QRP plaque and the Mobile CW Low Power Plaque.

New Business:

           1. It has been mentioned that we could save about $500.00 per year if we eliminate the phone line
              to the repeater. The autopatch is rarely used, but the phone line is needed for remote control of
              the repeater. After discussion, this item was tabled until after the three projects listed in “Old
              Business, Item 7” have been completed.

Announcements:

           1.   Next month’s program will be on emergency survival items. Presented by Greg & Carol Abbott.
           2.   Ozarkcon at Lebanon on April 4, 5, & 6.
           3.   The Missouri QSO Party is April 5 & 6.
           4.   Hamvention 2014 in Dayton is scheduled in May.
           5.   If you have not signed in, please do so before you leave the meeting.
