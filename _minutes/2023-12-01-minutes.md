                                     Minutes of the
                             Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.
                                   Jefferson City, MO

Meeting of: December 14, 2023

Due to the MMARC annual Christmas dinner, Tim Raymer – N0UI, MMARC President,
declared there would be no December 2023 Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club meeting.

Announcements:

   1. Mid-MO ARC Net – Weekly – (Wednesdays 8:00pm LOCAL time)
      147.00 MHz (-0.60Mhz Offset, 127.3Hz CTCSS Tone)
      442.150 MHz (+5.00Mhz Offset, 127.3Hz CTCSS Tone)

   2. Mid Mo Electronic Technical Group:
      First Tuesday of the month 7:00 PM at the Cole County Emergency Management Agency
      office, 1736 Southridge Drive in Jefferson City
      All other Tuesdays 7:00 PM – Zoom
      Jack Boswell - N0KSF or Josh Wood - W0ODJ

   3. Saturday Breakfast/CW Class:
      Saturday morning 8:00 AM club breakfast is located at: St. Mary’s Hospital 2505
      Mission Dr, Jefferson City.
      Enter through the Outpatient Entrance. Elevator or Stairs down one level to the cafeteria.

       Morse Code class: - Follows breakfast at 09:00 am.
       Family members and guests are always welcome.

   4. Missouri Emergency Services Net:
      Every Sunday at 2:00 PM local time
      3.963 MHz +/- with a backup of 7.263 MHz +/- depending on band conditions.

Upcoming Events:

   •   SLSRC Winterfest: January 27, 2024; Gateway Center, 1 Gateway Dr., Collinsville, IL

   •   Winter Field Day: January 27th & 28th, 2024. Gary Zeilman’s, K0WYN, farmhouse in
       Frankenstein is a possible location for our club.

Next Meeting: 7:00 PM January 11, 2024, at the Cole County Sheriff Training Room located at
350 E High Street, Jefferson City, MO
