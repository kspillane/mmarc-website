          Not Present. Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, and Bill Gerth, W4RK commented that the CW classes are an

          members more than just getting on the air.

Field Day:
             Mike Dolson, N0ZH, reporting: We don’t have our bonus points added up yet but we have a good
             chance of beating last year’s total. Sheriff White visited but he can’t be counted as both served
             agency and elected official. John Beetem was there but did not sign log-in sheet. Any comments
             should be e-mailed to Jerry Sill, NN0JS, at jsill25@mchsi.com

Old Business:

             1. Steve Hick advised that the Bike MS organization will provide hotel rooms for anyone who
                chooses to attend.
             2. If Bike MS requests our trackers, the Board has determined that a deposit covering the full
                replacement cost will be required.
             3. Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, has spoken to Sheriff White regarding a possible donation for use of the
                training room. The Sheriff prefers any donation go to his own training fund. Jim will provide
                additional details at our August meeting.

New Business:
         1. Steve Hick, WW0G, commented that he would like to make our old Field Day antennas and coax
              to new members who cannot afford new items. After discussion, a motion to approve was made
              by Steve Hick, WW0G, and seconded by Joe Betros, KB0QHZ, and carried. Other club owned
              items which are no longer being used should be recycled or offered to club members. It was
              suggested that a list of these items be compiled and discussed at a later meeting.
           2. J.D. Simmons, WA0BER, asked if the 2 meter nets should be considered ARES nets. After
              discussion, a motion to incorporate the 2 meter nets into ARES was made by Karl Schulte
              WA2KBZ, and seconded by Rick Bennett, KC0PET, and carried.
           3. The Linn club is handling SkyWarn.

Announcements:

           1. The Tech Group, which meets the first Tuesday of the month, is always looking for new
              attendees. The group meeting provides an excellent opportunity to observe the projects of other
              members and learn many technical aspects of our hobby.
           2. Night of Nights is July 12th beginning at 1700 Pacific time.
           3. Flight of the Bumblebees is July 26th. This is a QRP event. Skeeter Hunt follows on August 9th.
              The Skeeter Hunt is adding sideband to the event.
           4. J Gattermeir, W0QBX, has a quantity of coax available for any member interested.
           5. Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, advised that ARRL members can have an e-mail address through ARRL
              (ex: k9ztv@arrl.net) which will forward any e-mail to your normal address. One advantage of
              this is that if you change your e-mail address, all you have to do is change the setting on ARRL.
