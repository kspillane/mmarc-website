2-Meter Repeater report: Tim, KAØOUV, reports that the repeater is performing well.
There is some short-term work and long-term work to do on the repeater. Short-term work
includes upgrading the battery backup. Long-term things to think about include changing
feedline, antenna, and possible structure replacement.


Digipeater report: Rich, NØJBF, reports that the digipeater has been overhauled by Rick,
KCØPET, and is now back at it’s main location at the southwest campus of Capital Region
Hospital. Thanks to all who helped with the effort to get the site work completed.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN reported that things are
working well. The Centralia node is back up and running after being temporarily down during
the MS Bike Ride.


Local Emergency Coordinator's report: Rich, NØJBF, expressed thanks to all those who
helped out with the MS Bike Ride. Thanks were also given to Kent, K9ZTV, for heading up
the Starkenburg Scouting event.


District Emergency Coordinator’s report: Joe, KBØQHZ, reported that Osage County will
be having a training session on message handling. This will be on Tuesday evenings on the
Linn repeater.

VE Liaison’s report: No report.


CW Classes: Kent, K9ZTV, reported that the classes continue to meet as scheduled. If
anyone wishes to take over as the instructor for the advanced class, please let Kent know.
The advanced class meets at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings. The
intermediate class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am, while the
beginning class meets an hour later at 10am.


Training Committee: Rich, NØJBF, reported that the Technician class is going well. There
are about 15-16 students taking part. The most recent class covered basic electronics with
Rick, KCØPET, serving as the instructor.


Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Bryan, KDØGRU, and Laura, KDØGRV, brought some
samples to display. Caps will be $6.15, the jacket with a nylon lining will be $36.65. A
callsign can be added for $1.50. A jacket with a quilted lining will run $42.15. An order sheet
will be created and placed on the website. Those wishing to purchase items will need to print
off the order sheet, fill it out, and turn it in with your money.
