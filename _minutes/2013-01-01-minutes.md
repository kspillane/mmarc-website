            Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reported no problems.

         Digipeater Report:
            Rich Bennett, KC0PET, thanked those who helped reinstall the digipeater and reported that it
            was functioning properly. He added that its frequency is 145.01 MHz.

         Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN):
            Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, advised MEPN is functioning properly.

         Local Emergency Coordinator’s Report:
            Joe Betros, KB0QHZ, reporting – Status quo; we do have an ARES coordinator in Boone County.
            Joe will attend the next meeting in Columbia to present him with our certificate.

         VE Liaison’s Report:
            Nancie McAnaugh absent, Steve Hick, KD0GRM, reporting – 8 new Technician Licenses from the
            recent class. There was some confusion with one person who had two FN numbers but the
            situation was resolved with the social security number.

         CW Class:
           Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, reporting – the last two sessions were devoted to preparing for Straight
           Key Night including conversing across the table with each other; will probably continue that
           routine for a while. Kent also invited anyone interested in joining the intermediate class to come
           to Mel’s at 9:00 a.m. on Saturdays. Bill Gerth, W4RK, added that those who participated in
           Straight Key Night should get their reports in to HQ as soon as possible so their call sign will
           appear in the upcoming QST article. Also, include your affiliation to the club so that we get
           credit as well.

         Training Committee:
            Mike Thayer absent, Steve Hick, KD0GRM, reporting – There will be a General Class coming up.
            Details will be announced later.

         Club Jackets / Shirts / Patches:
            No report – Brian and Laura both absent.

         Mid-MO Emergency Radio Service:
            Nothing to report.

         Field Day 2013:
            Mike Dolson, N0ZH, reminded the members of Field Day scheduled for this coming June. He
            will be sending out letters to appropriate agencies in preparation.

New Business:

         Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, and Bill Gerth, W4RK, encouraged all members to consider going to the
         Dayton Hamfest this year, as well as the Four Days in May QRP (put on by QRP International) in
         Fairborn, Ohio. The latter incorporates very impressive seminars on many facets of amateur radio as
         well as vendor booths and presentations. Jack Boswell, N0KSF, added that QRP International also
         offers an excellent magazine.
          Mike Dolson, N0ZH, reported that Roger Barnard, WA0HAM, has collected numerous amateur radio
          related items over the years and would like to give them to someone who is likely to use them. The
          big item is a hand-built linear amplifier built to a QST manual. Anyone interested contact Mike or
          Roger.

          Karl Schulte, WA2KBZ, initiated a discussion about the length and redundancy of the 2 meter net
          script. Mike Dolson, N0ZH, commented on the history of the existing script and that he and Tom
          Hammond, N0SS, had rewritten it a while back to bring it into its current state. He told Karl that he
          was welcome to go over it and edit it some. Others commented that the script is mostly a suggestion
          that the net controller uses to include all necessary items, but can ad lib as they see fit. There was
          also a short discussion on how to handle formal net traffic – the consensus was that it be handled as
          in comes in rather than at the end of the net.

Announcements:
        None

The meeting was adjourned at 7:40 p.m. and was followed by a presentation by Karl Schulte, WA2KBZ. The
presentation included procedures, examples, and demonstrations of antique telegraph equipment and early radio
devices.

Compiled and Submitted by Bob Heald, W0XII
