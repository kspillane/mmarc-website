ARES Report:
        Jerry Sill, NN0JS, reporting. There was a SEMA exercise yesterday (September 9th) with the
        scenario of an F2 tornado in Sedalia. The Missouri Emergency Services Net was involved.
        Noise at the Fairgrounds interfered. We operated about 90 minutes. Members assisting as the
        JC Ring were Joe Betros, Jim Biggerstaff, Bill Gerth, Karl Schulte, and Kent Trimble. There
        was no digital or e-mail involved. Kent thought the exercise went very well. Some of the
        messages were very complex. Hank Taylor was at SEMA and said that the reception there was
        very good for a change. Jerry Sill was also at SEMA. The FLDigi worked very well. The
        forms could be attached to e-mail and/or saved as a .pdf. Jerry Sill will look into liaisons for
        Cole County Emergency Coordinator.

Old Business:

             1. Bike MS is this weekend in Illinois. Some members are planning to be there.
             2. We are still waiting for a call from Ameren regarding assistance with the antenna upgrade
                at our primary site.
             3. The Red Cross Smoke Detector Installation Program was carried out today (Sept 10th). Jim
                Biggerstaff, N0TKN, Ron Brizendine, KA0ZIY, Gordon Belcher, KE0CQV, and Kent
                Trimble, K9ZTV participated. We were divided into four groups. The Red Cross goal is
                to reduce the number of fire fatalities by 25% in five years. Kent advised that pushing the
                test button on a smoke detector does NOT test the detector, just its audible tone and
                battery and that a smoke detector is only good for about five years. Jim added that the Red
              Cross contacted families who had previously had fires to urge installation. The program
              focused on single family homes, not apartments.
           4. The Wednesday evening K0ETY nets are now ARES nets. When you acknowledge the net,
              please remember in addition to name, call sign, city, and any comments, indicate whether
              or not you are and ARES member.
