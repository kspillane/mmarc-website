        same night as our meeting.

CW Class:
             Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, reporting: Because of the SET exercise, there will be no classes on
             Saturday, but breakfast as usual for those not participating in SET.

Training Committee:
          Mike Thayer, KM0S, reporting: There will be a Technician class starting January 25th in the
          Sheriff’s Department training room. As previously, classes will be about two hours on
          Monday nights. We are requesting that the students come to our December meeting to pick up
          their books and pay the $25.00 cost. So far, 21 are signed up. The intent for this change is to
          get the students interested in the club. The December and January presentations will be
          geared toward new operators. The students represent several organizations in town including
          the Red Cross, DHSS, MHA, St. Mary’s Hospital, and others.

Field Day:
             Jerry Sill, NN0JS, reporting: Field Day scores were published in the last issue of QST. There
             were about 350 in our class and we were in the top 20% overall, and second in Missouri
             behind Raytown. Our score was just under 5000, about 2.5 times 2014 Field Day.

Old Business:

             1. W0O Special Event, Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, reporting: We had almost 500 QSOs, working
                all states & provinces and several European countries. I believe this was our best event.
                Kent complimented Karl Evans and Karl Schulte on their operations. Karen Pettit
                designed the QSL cards. They originally printed darker than we’d like, but Bill Gerth,
                W4RK, post-processed the graphic file, and we have ordered 125 copies for $18.00.
New Business:
         1. Nominations for Officers: Jack Renick, KD0AKF, is aging out as Board Director. There
             were no nominations from the floor. The Board made the following nominations: President
             – Jerry Sill, NN0JS, Vice President – Andy Adams, WN0I, Secretary/Treasurer – Mike
             McCrady, N0ESE, Director – Bob Heald, W0XII. Nominations will be reopened at the
             December meeting.
         2. There will be a garage sale for Gregg Abbott’s items; December 12th is the tentative date.
             There are lots of antennas, parts, etc. This will be a cash-only sale. The sale is expected to
             take a couple of hours in the morning. An e-mail will follow with a list of the items. Later
             on, there will be a sale of test equipment based on club invitations only, and still later, a
             sale of other radio equipment.
