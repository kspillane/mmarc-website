An exercise is planned for Thursday, May 19 at Capital Region hospital from 10am to

help.

Kent, K9ZTV, and Bill, W4RK, made a trip to Branson to check the operating site the NLE.

CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reported that the advanced class has been sparse the last few
weeks. They are concentrating on sending good CW. This class meets at Mel’s Country
Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings.

Kent, K9ZTV, reported that in his absence, Bill, W4RK, has been covering the intermediate
and beginning classes. The class is progressing and some folks have been getting on the
air making live QSO’s. Field Day prep will be coming up. The intermediate class meets at
Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am, while the beginning class meets an
hour later at 10am.


Training Committee: Tom, NØSS, reported that the upgrade class has been going well.


Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Bryan, KDØGRU, reported that the embroidered patches
for jackets need to be larger than the one used for the t-shirts so he is waiting on pricing
info. Once that is done, the jacket pricing will be finalized and information can be placed on
the club website for all the various items.
