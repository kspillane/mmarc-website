     Significant changes to the repeater system. Reviewed by Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN.

      switches temporarily fixes the issues. Can replace DIP Switches or hardwire. Primary
      repeater needs to be moved back to the Linn Tower Site. Report that Linn repeater can
      be accessed from Fayette.
