Miscellaneous Items:
   1.     Elections were held last month – congratulations to all officers.
   2.     Mike Dolson, N0ZH, resigned the position of 2 meter manager and was succeeded by
          Steve Hick, WW0G.
   3.     Bill Gerth, W4RK, was elected as Board Member replacing outgoing Member, Steve Hick,
          WW0G.
   4.     We will operate for the ARRL 100th Anniversary for the week starting May 28th
          (Wednesday to Wednesday). We’ll have more details later. Operation will be in the
          General bands and can be sideband and CW simultaneously. A coordinator is needed for
          the event – Mitch Odneal will volunteer if nobody else wants it.
   5.     Bill Gerth, W4RK, reporting on the recent hospital emergency exercise – yesterday
          (January 8th) all hospitals in Region F (same as MSHP Troop F) participated in the
          exercise. Region F was the first in the state to conduct this exercise; there will be
          additional exercises at later dates. It was the first use of amateur radios in the emergency
          trailers. There were four trailers involved: two in Columbia, one each at Capitol Region
          and Lake Region. Only two, Boone Hospital and Capitol Region worked; the other two
          had problems. Bill is the liaison for Capitol Region Medical Center and Dave Bagge,
          KD0CCP, ran the net. The exercise was followed by a conference call to critique the
          exercise. The Missouri Hospital Association was very complimentary, however, there are
          lots of things to work out before additional exercises. The main issue is personnel and
          organizational structure.
   6.     Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, reporting: the Boy Scouts have asked us to assist with radio merit
          badge efforts on March 15th. The program will involve a maximum of ten scouts aged 12
          & 13. They must carry on a ten minute QSO, real or simulated, on CW, Voice, or Digital.
                      Club participants must be child safety certified. The location will be announced later and
                      additional details will be mostly by e-mail.

              vanity call sign “W9ABD” of which Bill is the Trustee. The call sign “9ABD” was originally
              assigned to Willis Porter Corwin who operated from his parent's residence on East McCarty
              Street as a teenager and who participated in the first transcontinental relay of formal message
              traffic on the night of January 27, 1917. Willis is the first known ham operator in Jefferson City
              and the first resident to volunteer for military duty during World War I. He did so in response to
              a QST article about the U.S. Navy's urgent need for wireless operators. Also, several club
