New Business:
         The News Tribune is putting together a publication regarding the role of amateur radio during the
         1993 flood. Anyone who was involved, please let them know.

          Volunteers are still needed for the Bike MS which is September 7th and 8th.

          Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ, suggested that a booth at the State Fair would be a good idea. Volunteers
          would talk about the benefits and enjoyment of amateur radio. He would like to get other clubs
          involved in the activity so volunteers would only have to commit to a single day. After extensive
          favorable discussion, Tony agreed to talk to the Sedalia club and report back next meeting.

          A list of club activities should be added to our website. To be determined.

          Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, suggested that the club’s emergency coordinator should put some thought

          from where.
