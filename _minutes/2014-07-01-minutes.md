Field Day:
             Jerry Sill, NN0JS, reporting: I am extremely pleased with the outcome. We’ll work harder next
             year for sideband operators. There were a lot of really good people doing things for other members.
             We had several media representatives, area officials, and other notables visiting as well as many
             other visitors. I’ll try to submit our entry form next week. We made 584 QSOs, including 14
             digitally, and 41 GOTA contacts. There were a lot of unlicensed youth operators. We got quite a
             few bonus points as a result. There were some antenna issues. Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, suggests an
             ARRL 100th Anniversary message would be nice to send to all members.
