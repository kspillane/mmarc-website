                                     Minutes of the

                                 April 18     W4RK         8

  2-meter Repeater report: The repeater has had noise problems. The issue appears to be
  due to sensitivity problems. NØSS and KAØOUV to investigate.

  Digipeater report: No problems noted.

  MEPN: Antennas have been installed, radio and other equipment to be installed later.
  KCØPET and KCØLZG to host a program about packet at a future meeting.
  Emergency Coordinators report: KCØPET discussed the Missouri New Madrid
  earthquake exercise to be held June 19 – 21. The club discussed ham communications in
  the event an earthquake did happen.

  CW Class: The CW class continues to meet every Saturday, at 0645 (before breakfast), at
  Mel’s Country Café.

  License class: NØSS reported that 3 members of the class took and passed technician
  license exams. Three or four others to test in the future.


  New business:

  Field Day 2007: Porite site has been secured. MEROC, DPS and new command vehicle
  are lined up.

  Easter Seals Walk: KØJNW will be involved with the Easter Seals walk/run on May 19.
  He needs 6 volunteers to provide communication support. A few have volunteered already.

  MO QSO Party: Participants in the 2007 Missouri QSO Party (NØSS, W4RK, NNØB,
  WØESE, etc) discussed their experiences in the contest.

  NNØB to move: Steve announced that he will be moving to Silver City, New Mexico after
  Field Day. Steve will resign as MMARC Vice President at that time.

  WØS: Kent, K9ZTV discussed the WØS special event station to be held in Branson April
  14-15. The event is to commemorate the 95th anniversary of the Titanic tragedy. Kent will
  be driving to Branson to participate and invited any club members to join him.

  Willis: Kent, K9ZTV had an ARRL diamond member brick made in honor of Willis P.
  Corwin (9ABD).

  VE exams: John Munsterman

  Program: none

Respectfully submitted: Bryan Braunschweig (WØESE), Secretary-Treasurer.
