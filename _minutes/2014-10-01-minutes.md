Old Business:

          1. Zonta 5K – Carol Abbott, KD0YBL, said it would have been nice to acknowledge transmissions
             so operators know they were getting out. If problems are suspected, a radio check is appropriate.
             Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ, followed the last person back to Memorial Park.
          2. W1AW/0 – Andy Adams, WN0I, & Mike Dolson, N0ZH, made about 83 QSOs in an hour.
          3. UHF Project – Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting: We have put our installation on hold; a
             lighted cross has been placed in the antenna arrays, so all antennas have to be moved. In the
             meantime, the UHF repeater will be installed at the current St. Mary’s facility.
          4. Joe Betros, KB0QHZ, reported on the Simplex test at the new St. Mary’s location: the test went
             extremely well considering the ongoing thunderstorm. We hit Clark, MO, about 20 miles north
             of Columbia and about an equal distance to the south.
New Business:

           1. Reminder that the Frankenstein Special Op will again be held on October 31st. Antennas will be
              erected on Saturday, October 25th leaving Sunday as a rain date. We’ll meet at the Eastland
              McDonalds at 1300. Joe Betros, KB0QHZ, has volunteered his K3 for use at the sideband station.
              If you are working W0O, make sure you get the incoming call sign correct. Those wanting a
              QSL card must submit a SASE. Make sure you are transmitting in the General range.
           2. Tracker batteries: Jim Biggerstaff, N0TKN, reporting. We borrow the trackers from Moniteau
              County. Three batteries need replacing at a cost of about $77.00 for all three at Dark Horse.
              Karl Schulte, WA2KBZ, made a motion that we make the purchase and was seconded by Leonard
              Neff, KD0YLW. After Steve Hick, WW0G, said that he would donate two batteries, the motion
              was amended to purchase one battery and was carried.
           3. Next month we will make nominations for officers. All nominations are welcome. Jim
              Biggerstaff, N0TKN, will be outgoing as a director, so nominations are required to fill his
              position. Make sure you nominate someone who is willing to serve and will attend meetings.

Announcements:

           1. Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, advised that Jim Houston, W0OID, passed away recently. He had a bunch
              of tubes and other equipment. If you hear of any similar situations and family just wants to get
              rid of the equipment, contact other club members who may want some or all of it to preserve said
              items.
           2. Karl Shulte, WA2KBZ, noted that Islands on the Air qualifies for awards with a contact.
