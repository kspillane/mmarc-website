2-Meter Repeater report: Jim, NØTKN, reported that the new battery and uninterruptible
power supply (UPS) were purchased and installed. The total price for all the new hardware
came in under budget at $783.75. Upon a power failure, the repeater will switch to low power
and add a tail message to indicate the repeater is being powered by the UPS. If power is not
restored and the UPS shuts down, the repeater will then switch to a second tail message to
indicate it is being powered by the backup battery
