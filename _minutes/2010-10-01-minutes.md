2-Meter Repeater report: Tom, NØSS reported that the repeater was down for 24 hours
recently. The battery pack for the UPS died. A power strip was purchased to get the
repeater back online, but there is currently no backup power. The Technical Committee and
officers will meet later to discuss options.

Last year the club was given several 12V, 75Ah batteries and these were distributed to club
members that expressed an interest. If you find that you are not using them or do not have
a current use for them and would like to donate them back to the club, please let Tom know.


Digipeater report: Rick, KCØPET, reported that little has changed since last month. There
were several offers for enclosures and one that Kent offered will work. There are still some
odd parts needed but should be easily sourced and/or donated. The digipeater is currently
off the air and is still a work in progress at this time.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that the 6-meter
transmitter took a lightning strike. The receiver portion functions ok, but there is currently
no transmit operation. You can still make a connection through Centralia to St. Louis.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ, reported that nothing has been going
on with the local EC. The Osage County EOC now has the callsign W0SGE and has asked
for help getting a packet station set up. Rich, NØJBF, will be covering for Joe while Joe is
out of town from 10/25 through 11/4.


VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported there are no exams scheduled. Nancie
currently has the VE materials.


CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reported that the advanced class is doing well. Kent wrote and
sent some messages for the students at a recent class. This class meets before breakfast
at 6:45am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café.

Mike, WAØZOG, reported that the intermediate class was conducted by Bill and the
students were working on 3 and 4 letter words at 20wpm. After receiving, the students
switched to sending 3 and 4 letter words. This class meets after breakfast from 9-10am at
Mel’s.

Rich, NØJBF, reported that he and Andy handled the beginning class and had two people
attending. They did a follow-up of the intermediate class by sending 3 and 4 letter words
around 15wpm. This class meets at Mel’s at 10am.
