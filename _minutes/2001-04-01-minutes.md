                               Minutes of the
                       Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.
                             Jefferson City, MO




Meeting of: April 12, 2001


Meeting called to order by Kurt Bleich (KBØHNR)

In attendance:  KØDXR, NØSS, N9OOC, NØMV, KAØOUV, K9ZTV, KCØJAL, KCØCZI,
    KCØEYG, KBØVGO, NØEDM, KCØJTK, KØWYN, KØZBO, WØJES, NØVGX, KCØIFL,
    NØOFD, KØJJS, NMØX, WNØX, KBØHNR, KBØQHZ, KØEMT, WØRAJ

Date/location of the next meeting:  May 10, 2001.  Central United Church of
    Christ, corner of Washington and W. Ashley Streets.   Enter from the
    alley on the east side of the church (midway up the alley).

Minutes:  March 2001 minutes were unavailable and were tabled for
discussion until the next meeting.

Bank balance a/o April 11, 2001 is $1921.04

Old business:

2 Meter Net Report:  Not recorded.

Dual Band Antenna Purchase:  Motion made and seconded, voted to pass, to
    reimburse KBØHNR for the purchase price of the antenna.

Jim Peasner Easter Memorial:  Voted to approve the annual Easter Memorial
for Jim Peasner.

Earth Day Report:  Map of Earth Day location was passed around and
    discussed.  KCØCZI will furnish tent that is self-supporting.  Several
    members plan to check out the set-up location.  Participation in the
    event will begin at 9:00 a.m. but others could be there earlier to help
    set up the tent.  Bryan (KØEMT) reported that tables have been located,
    electric is nearby and the club has extension cords, no need to bring
    QSL cards, sign-up sheet available for classes, club banner may be
    displayed.  Need to have posters on propagation and current events that
    are weather-related.  Expenses to have materials scanned and/or printed
    were approved.

1 5/8” Hardline:  Need to find time to inventory the line at Tom’s (NØSS)
    QTH on the following Saturday.  NØQBO (Luke) in Osage Beach would like
    200 feet for a 220 repeater.  Several club members were instrumental in
    acquiring this material, so it would be preferable to have club members
    get first pick of the material.  This needs to be done quickly so that
    the reels can be removed from Tom’s yard.










New business:

Field Day Update:  The letter from SEMA allowing the use of their vehicle
    is waiting to be signed by the administration.  Resource vehicle is
    also available to use as information distribution.  In the process of
    getting approval from Maytag.

Use of SEMA Equipment:  May offer to operate the Missouri Traffic Net on an
    occasional basis to give the equipment some exercise and familiarize
    club members with its use.  KCØCZI asked to see hands of anyone
    interested in participating in this activity.  Missouri Traffic Net is
    on 3.960 MHz at 5:45 p.m. using Richard’s call sign, NØQOI.





2 Meter Net schedule:        March 18   NØMV
                 April 25         KAØOUV
                 May 2       KBØHNR
                 May 9, 2001 KCØCZI

Hand-held Device legislation:  Would limit the use of any telecommunication
    device while driving a vehicle to emergency personnel and CB operators.
     Representative Holland told KCØCZI that the bill seems to be “dead” in
    committee and will not go to the general session.  The bill may be
    brought up again in future years.  WØJAS has quite a lot of additional
    information available.


Dues received at (or since) meeting:  KØIRI, KCØCZI, WØRAJ




Respectfully submitted (05/07/2001): Steven C. Klenc, KCØACQ, Secretary-
Treasurer


Visit the MMARC web page at: http://www.mmccs.com/mmarc


Electronic copies e-mailed to those who have expressed interest and for
whom e-mail addresses are known.  If you wish to receive the MMARC Meeting
Minutes, please send a message to klenc@earthlink.net  asking to be added
to the mailing list.
