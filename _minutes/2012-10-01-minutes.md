Rich, NØJBF, announced that Kent had sent him some information about a new amateur
satellite in orbit. It is called FITSAT-1 and operates on 437.250Mhz. It is the size of a 10cm
cube. It has LED’s on the outside which flash a message in Morse code and will be visible at
night using binoculars.
