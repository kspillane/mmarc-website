Local Emergency Coordinator's report: Rich, NØJBF, reported that the exercise last
month of simulating an EF-5 tornado hitting Springfield went well. Thanks to those who
helped pass message traffic. There were around 60 messages relayed.

There is new online training available from the ARRL in the areas of Emergency
Communication (EmComm) and ARES. The classes are $20 each and you can take them at
your leisure. Joe will check to see if those that already passed the Level 1 course can get
credit.


District Emergency Coordinator’s report: Joe, KBØQHZ, reported no updates.


VE Liaison’s report: Nancie, NMØC, reported all that took the Extra exam from the training
class passed. No new exams are currently scheduled.


CW Classes: Kent, K9ZTV, reported no updates.

The advanced class meets at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings. The
intermediate class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am. The
beginning class meets at 10am.


Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that six students took and passed the Extra
exam following the end of the class. Three of those were current club members. A
Technician class is being considered for the fall after school starts. Instructors will be
needed.


Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: Laura, KDØGRV, reported that the patches are now in and
they will run $4.50 each. The patches were given to Mark, KIØY, who will have them
available at meetings for those interested. Bryan, KDØGRU, was to have an update on the
jackets, but was not available at the meeting.

MMARC meeting.
