2 Meter Repeater Report:  Power failure of 1½ hours caused the repeater to
    go off the air after the           batteries in the back-up supply ran
    down.  Once the AC power returned, the repeater was back on and
    functioning normally.


    We are hearing several local area police departments on the repeater.
    The cause is most likely someone with a scanner re-broadcasting through
    the repeater or a dual-band radio piping back in.  This does not seem
    to be a problem when the PL tone is operational.

APRS Report:  Digipeater is on and functioning.


    MS 150:  Ben (NØOFD) reported that there were more than 40 operators
    that assisted with the event this year and it went very well.
    Operators from Columbia, Moberly, Mexico, and other locations also
    joined in the effort this year.  Tom (NØSS), Kurt (KBØHNR), and Rich
    (WNØX) all deserve a special thanks for their efforts time spent
    helping to coordinate the logistics.
    There were some locations along the route that had poor reception of
    the base station and this is thought to have something to do with nulls
    coming off the multi-band antennas and the fact that we are only
    operating from a short mast tower.  Operated on low power the first
    day, but increased to 110 watts on day two.
    There seemed to be a lack of manpower at some of the rest stops.  Ben
    is making a list of the problems and deficiencies and will keep them
    for implementing improvements next year.


    GPS trackers for the MS 150 did have a few problems.  The system became
    overloaded at times and the program was set up to do some complicated
    things with the data coming in.  Next year it will be kept more simple
    and basic so the essential information will get back to the net
    control.


    A rather lengthy discussion of medical emergency preparedness followed
    and included the use (and non-use) of SEMA radios and police radios for
    Mutual Aid.


Jamboree on the Air:  The event is being coordinated by Bryan (KØEMT).  It
    will be held in Lorie, MO on Saturday October 20.  Demonstrations of
    APRS, ATV, backpack portable HF, mobile HF, base HF, and home brew HF
    are planned.  A fox hunt may also be included if time permits.  The
    call sign for this special event will be KØS.


Halloween Special Event:  Call sign will be WØO and will be transmitting
    from Frankenstein, MO on the night of October 31st (a Wednesday).
    Announcements will be made in the ARRL newsletter and e-mail lists.


    New business:

Emergency Response to Sep 11th Events:  Kevin (KCØCZI) and Kurt (KBØHNR)
    sent out an e-mail to all ARES members requesting to standby for
    possible activation.  Within two hours there were 9-10 operators on the
    air in both Cole and Moniteau Counties.  Kevin contacted the Red Cross
    to offer our assistance with handling health and welfare (H&W)
    messages, but was informed that there would be a 48-72 hour moratorium
    on those types of services.  He also offered that ARES could help to
    receive the calls that might come in.  There were nine different nets
    operating on various HF and VHF frequencies.


    This lead to a discussion of how to make sure a message that is
    trafficked gets into the right hands… if it is possible for us to
    ensure that does happen… or even if it is our concern or obligation as
    amateurs.  This is one of the topics that will be discussed as a result
    of this emergency.  There was also some concern about which of several
    frequencies should be monitored.  A designated frequency for future
    needs will be identified as a result of these discussions, but it would
    be best to monitor 147.00 MHz unless directed otherwise.


    It was suggested by Kent (K9ZTV) that a list of mobile operators be
    made available in case all electric power should be lost in this area.
    These mobile operators would be one of the few methods of communicating
    with government and private agencies.  A resource net needs to be
    developed.

Discussion of Roster:  It was requested that a roster of club members be
    made available and furnished to all other club members.  This list
    would provide names, call signs, addresses, e-mail addresses, and
    possibly list the equipment the member owns.  It was also suggested
    that this list be posted on the club web page, but it was pointed out
    that this may involve matters of privacy protection.  It was also
    mentioned that this information is kept by the club secretary and is
    made available several months prior to Field Day.
                             Minutes conclude at this point due to end of
    tape.
