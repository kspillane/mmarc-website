CW Class:
  Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, Night of Nights is scheduled for tomorrow night (July 12th). This is a
  commemorative event sponsored by the Maritime Radio Historical Society and is held on July
  12th every year for the shut down of facilities at Point Reyes, California. Was in operation for
  many decades monitoring 500 kilocycles which is the maritime calling frequency. The event
  runs from 5pm to 2am Central Time. Their call sign is K6KPH. All are encouraged to visit their
  website.

Training Committee:
