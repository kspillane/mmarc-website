K0ETY Repeater Report: Jim Biggerstaff – N0TKN

   •   Jim reported that the repeater is working good. 1004 PTT’s this past month.

Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): 145.070 MHz:

   •   Jim reported MEPN working good. He connected out to Americus and Columbia. The
       network goes no further East than Americus. Jim noted that he had received a packet
       message from a ham in Moberly through MEPN. He also noted that the network is not
       used very often but is an available digital link (i.e. digipeater) available to all as well as a
       Winlink node.

CW Class: Kent Trimble – K9ZTV

   •   Kent thanked Bryan, W0ESE, for filling in this past month during Kent and Bill,
       W4RK’s absence. Bryan indicated that things went well during the class.

   •   Kent described a recent QSO with a ham in Illinois that involved white shirts and ties,
       funeral homes, and President George Bush. Kent noted that you never know what a QSO
       conversation might end up discussing. He noted that in SOTA and POTA
       “conversations”, for example, the minimal information shared does not give the operators
       a chance to get to know each other.
