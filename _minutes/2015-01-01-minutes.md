CW Class:
             Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, and Bill Gerth, W4RK, reporting: We have been practicing with straight keys
             for several weeks because of Straight Key Night coming up December 31st.
             A. W9ABD was activated by six operators including one student from CW class. We had over 50
                 contacts. Several others used their own call. QSL cards were passed out to those present who
                 made contacts. Mike Dolson, worked several who were using old equipment.

Training Committee:
          Mike Thayer, KM0S, reporting: We will have a General Class sometime this spring. More
          instructors will be needed.

Field Day:
             Jerry Sill, NN0JS, reporting: I’d like to start preparing for this year’s event. I’ll line up the trailers
             by the first part of February. March 1st, we’ll ask for major equipment. April and May, minor
             equipment plans. I’d like to have some of our new people actively involved. The event is scheduled
             for June 27th and 28th.

Old Business:

             1. Kent passed out QSL cards for all who participated in any way with the W0O event.
             2. With one exception, all officers were reelected for another term. The exception was Jim
                Biggerstaff, N0TKN, who completed his three-year term as Director and was succeeded by Kent
                Trimble, K9ZTV. Thanks, Jim, for your valuable help over the past three years.
New Business:
         1. The Board of Directors of the Mid-MO Emergency Radio Service has dissolved that service.
              The Board felt it was no longer needed due to the fact that our served agencies have their own
              equivalent. Paperwork requirements were such that no one wanted to devote the time necessary
              to maintain it. It never accomplished what it was set up to do – namely give a tax incentive for
              donations.
         2. Tony DeWitt, AD0DQ, is moving out of the area and has resigned his duties as Cole Co. ARES
              Coordinator. We wish Tony all the best as he relocates to Alabama.

Announcements:

           1. February program: DXpedition – John Patterson, WC0W, presenting.
           2. We need a program for March and following months. Anyone interested, contact Mike Dolson,
              N0ZH.
           3. Collinsville Winterfest is Saturday, January 24th. For those interested in caravaning, we will
              leave the Sears south parking lot at 0700 a.m.
           4. Mike Dolson, N0ZH, challenged the membership to do something different this year; something
              that you’ve never done or haven’t done for a long time.
           5. The monthly 6 meter lunch has been resumed at Oscar’s. Our first get-together is scheduled for
              January 14th at 1100 in Oscar’s banquet room.

Adjourn:      2030
