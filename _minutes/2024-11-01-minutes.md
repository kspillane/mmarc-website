Meeting of: November 14, 2024


Presentation:

   •   Field Day Results— Tim showed the Field Day results for Class 2A. The club finished
       37th out of 286 entries in Class 2A. Tim mentioned that there were not enough operators
       to operate the phone/digital stations although Kent pointed out that phone and CW
       contacts were up 4% from last year with digital down from last year.

   •   Frankenstein—Kent reported 17 people attended. There were 601 contacts and 78 QSL’s
       sent out so far for those QSL’s received. The CW rig band pass filter was a problem and
       was later fixed by Tim. Tim explained what happened to the bandpass filter. The filter
       lost track of information from the decoder. With help from the factory, Tim was able to
       get the filter reset properly. Of the 601 QSOs, CW QSOs were 101 and phone contacts
       were 500. Kent noted that Tim and Mitch did good work on phone. Kent also
       complimented Mitch, Dave Beck, John Graves, Jim Biggerstaff, and Mike Smith for help
       with the antennas. Becky Widener and Ann Harvey were complemented for the fine food
       too. Kent mentioned that the radios all performed well. Bryan-W0ESE was
       complemented by Bill- W4RK for his use of a “bug” to send CW during the event. Tim
       mentioned working both a maritime mobile and an aeronautical mobile station.

Upcoming Events

    •   Winterfest, Collingsville, Il January 18, 2024

        Tim pulled up the website on the computer and there was more discussion on the
        Winterfest forums that will take place including a forum on the IARU Special Event that
        MMARC members participated in. Tim provided discussion of his and other MMARC
        member’s operation in the IARU event.

        It should be noted that there is another ham radio event (W9DYV Symposium)
        beginning on January 17, 2024. The Winterfest website will link to that event with both
        events at the Gateway Convention Center. Several club members have stayed overnight
        previously and attended both sets of events.

    •   ARRL 10 Meter Contest, Saturday, December 14 at 0000 UTC to December 15 at
        2359 UTC.

    •   SAQ Grimeton Radio Station (VLF frequency 17.2 Khz), December 24, 2024 at 0725
        UTC (You Tube live stream begins) with Startup of the Alexanderson Alternator SAQ
        at 0730 UTC. Transmission begins at 0800 UTC. The amateur radio station SK6SAQ
        will be operating on various frequencies (CW and SSB) during the day. If you have
        never seen the Alexanderson Alternator Startup, it is a sight to behold. Older You Tube
        videos exist if you miss this event.

    •   Jim- N0TKN discussed the International Space Station (ISS) passing over our area
        tomorrow night (11/15/24) for approximately 6 minutes at 5:40 pm running Southwest
        to Northeast. If conditions permit, the ISS will be easily visible. There is a ham radio
        cross band repeater on the ISS. Tim noted that the AMSAT website contains
        information about the availability of the various ham radio equipment on board.
        Thanks Jim.
