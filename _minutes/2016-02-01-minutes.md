ARES Report:
        Jerry Sill, NN0JS, reporting for Cole County: Instituted an Advisory Council consisting of Bill
        Gerth, W4RK and Mike Dolson, N0ZH. A suggestion was proposed by the council to have some
        training in May to go over Go Kits and portable equipment for use in emergencies. People attending
        would bring whatever portable equipment they have which would be reviewed by knowledgeable
        people at the training.

             J. D. Simmons, WA0BER, reporting for the ARES District F: There has been a recent interest by
             various counties to encourage more members to sign up for ARES. Also there has been interest in
             and testing of the FSQ mode for future use in emergency communications.

CW Class:
             Kent Trimble, K9ZTV, and Bill Gerth, W4RK, stated classes are going well. There is some
             consideration for starting a beginner’s CW class.

Training Committee:
          Bill Gerth, W4RK, reporting: Classes going well. About 20 students. One self studied and passed
          the Technician test.

Field Day:
             Andy Adams, WN0I, reporting: Planning continuing. The same location has been reserved and the
             same radio setup is anticipated. 2016 Field Day rules changes were reviewed and it was reported
             that they will not affect our efforts.
Old Business:

           1. Greg Abbott Sale: The sale set for February 13 has been postponed. An announcement will be
              made when a new date is determined.
           2. Mike McCrady, N0ESE, discussed the new e-mail process with Google Groups. The new
              process seems to be working. At least one person was not getting the e-mails. Mike McCrady
              will determine what the issue is.

New Business:
         1. Contribution to Sheriff’s Training Fund. It was proposed by the Board that the club contribute
             $250 to the Sheriff’s Training Fund. This will help fund the activities of the Sheriff’s Training
             Fund and is in appreciation of the Sheriff’s Office’s support of the MMARC in the use of its
             facilities for MMARC activities. Personnel discussing the proposal were supportive. The
             proposal was submitted, seconded and passed by voice vote.

Announcements:

           1. The Tech Group, which meets the first Tuesday of the month, is always looking for new
              attendees. The next meeting is March 1.
           2. The monthly luncheon, currently held at Oscar’s, is the third Wednesday of the month.
           3. Our next club meeting is March 10th.
           4. The 4 States QRP Event in Branson is scheduled for April 1-2.
           5. Dayton Hamvention is coming up in May.
           6. Karl Schulte, WA2KBZ, announced that there will be a solar eclipse whose path will travel
              through Jefferson City in August of 2017.
