Meeting of: May 9, 2024

Presentation:

       Recent QSOs:

          •     Craig- N0JN recently worked the Special Event Station at the Titan Missile
                Museum near Tucson, Arizona. This was a one day Special Event operation.
                Craig also mentioned working the DX Commander, the manufacturer of multi
                band push up vertical antennas known best for portable operations. The DX
                Commander is located in Central England and usually operates on Fridays.

          •     Rich- N0EAX mentioned his “success” with one way “contacts” with the Reverse
                Beacon Net. No one else responded to the CQs. Kent- K9ZTV reminded all to
                keep calling CQ.

          •     Mitch- NWOM worked the Florida QSO Party two weeks ago and was able to
                contact all sixty seven Florida counties. Congratulations Mitch!

       Field Day:

       Bryan- W0ESE reported on the Field Day availability of Callaway County Fire Stations
       for a Field Day location. Kent indicated that fields were large enough for our antennas,
       bathroom facilities available, a room available, and the Fire Department personnel want
       our involvement with them. Bryan also inquired about using the north end of the Cole
       County Fairgrounds but no response has been received. The use of the JCFD trailer is
       uncertain also. Jim- N0LBY discussed the possibility of using Jim (N0DVM) Crago’s
       property that has antenna space, towers, air conditioned facilities, bathroom facilities, and
       an RV. Tim- N0UI mentioned the availability of the Missouri Highway Patrol Command
       vehicle if needed. Art- N0JBF volunteered to be the Safety and Public Relations
       Captains. Kent and Bill- W4RK are the CW Captains, Mitch the Phone Captain, Tim and
       Jim- N0LBY the VHF Captains. Mitch indicated that the club laptops are fine for
       N1MM logging but the digital operators should probably bring their own laptops. Jim
       Crago invited the club to his property for Field Day and the club accepted the invitation.
        There was some discussion brought up by Art of operating a digital station. Jim- N0LBY
        mentioned last year’s setup of a phone station next to the digital station and the operator
        selects which to operate. Bryan mentioned still needing a volunteer for the GOTA
        Captain. Kent mentioned using Groups I/O to spread the word about Field Day and the
        availability of the GOTA Station to operators who infrequently operate.
