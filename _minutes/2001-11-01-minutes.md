2 Meter Repeater Report:

Digi-peater Report:  We are still getting erratic and sporadic interference
    on the repeater.  KAØOUV (Tim) suspects now that it may be caused by a
    faulty LNB on a satellite dish within a short distance of the repeater
    tower.  However, report of a bad video amplifier as far as a mile away
    has been known to get picked up by a repeater.  All indications are
    that the repeater is working OK and that the interference is not coming
    from ‘radio hill’.  Pattern of timing may be indicative of the viewing
    habits of the person with the dish.  Possibly using several DF units
    will help to isolate the location of the source by triangulation.


Emergency Coordinator Report:  Events of September 11th have brought home
    the need to improve our emergency coordination efforts.  We need to
    find things to do that will not replicate the efforts of ARES, but will
    compliment them.  Should be a community process, not relying on a few
    members, to ensure longevity.  What will be the objectives?  Who will
    be served agencies?  We can provide back-up support with manpower for
    ARES.  A membership questionnaire form should be developed to help
    build the needed data base.  Should coordinate with SEMA, other
    counties, local hospitals, Red Cross, and law enforcement agencies.  We
    need to let the agencies know what we have available as a service and
    let them decide how they could use our services.  It would be best if
    our assistance could be allocated through the SEMA offices.
    Coordinated training efforts.  Help to draft Emergency Plan in
    conjunction with ARES.

      No emergencies during the month, no activation required.




Jamboree on the Air:  KØEMT (Bryan) reported that there were 4 stations on
    the air and every scout that wanted to was able to talk.  They worked
    the ISS and a ham on a jet at 30,000 feet.  For next year we could use
    a few more operators, especially at the CW table were the scouts get to
    send some code.  Some scouts even returned in the afternoon for more
    ‘radio activity’.  JOTA is what “field day” is all about!  Special
    congratulations to Bryan.

Field Day Report:  We went from 25th to 12th out of about 500 entering
    clubs.  Kansas City club was not much higher than us and we beat St.
    Louis again.  Our equipment is not our advantage because we have always
    placed in the top 25.  Our antenna set-up was better than ever, and the
    results were apparent.  Perhaps we were not as aggressive on the phone
    bands as we could be.  It was suggested that we could improve our
    skills by participating in more contests throughout the year.   We
    should all be more active and work some of the many contests that are
    held.  There is one almost every weekend.  Captains need to be
    appointed sooner so they can formulate a plan and operator schedule,
    make equipment schedules, etc.


New business:

Kevin (KCØCZI) is putting together a multi-operator station to work the
