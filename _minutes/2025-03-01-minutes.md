K0ETY Repeater Report: Jim Biggerstaff – N0TKN
   Club has two new 100Ah Lithium Iron Phosphate Batteries, donated by John Glascock,
     N0IB. Please be sure to thank John for his donation to the club. Repeater is having some
     kind of issue. Had a lot of noise on Receive signals on the net. Found statistics look as
     normal. Could be transmission line or antenna. Issue seems to be worse when it is
     windy. Want to catch a windy day and see if we see changes. Joe Davis has a test
     (Desense test) that may help identify the issue. Harness may be difficult to replace. Had
     issues at MDC with replacing antenna in the past. May want to consider DB-222.
     Shorter antenna. PTT Count indicated 57 PTTs due to reset on equipment. If issues are
     noted on the 2m repeater, move to UHF.
