Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that there was an
outage recently where the 6M node would not connect to anything. A site visit was made
and the system reset. Since then there has not been any other problems noted.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ reported that there was a meeting with
FEMA to determine communications capabilities of various organizations across the state in
the case of an emergency. There will be no help for ARES as it is considered a private
entity, however, they know ARES is available.
                                            rd
There is an exercise scheduled for June 3 . ARES has not been formally requested to do
anything. It is only for Districts E and C.


VE Liaison’s report: Tom, NØSS, reported that the VE Liaison position is still open and
would prefer that someone step in and fill this role. Tom will continue to fill in for the time
being. An email will be sent around to all VE’s currently on the mailing list to find out if they
wish to remain active.


CW Classes: Tom, NØSS, reports that the advanced class is going well. This class meets
before breakfast at 6:45am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café. Thanks to Mike
Miller and Mike McCrady for getting the battery powered amplifiers into the area. Tom has
the 1/8” stereo jacks in hand for modifying keyers. Those who wish to have their keyers
modified, please get them to Tom.

Bill, W4RK, reports that the intermediate class is also going well with both students and
instructors having fun. They have been concentrating on sending and the club should have
more CW operators for Field Day. This class meets after breakfast from 9-10am at Mel’s.

Kent, K9ZTV, added that the new beginning class is doing well and students are now
between 5-10wpm. This class meets at 10am after breakfast and the slow speed class.

Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported the Extra study group is going well.

The Technician class is currently planned for three Saturdays: April 24, May 1, and May 8.
Instructors are still needed and anyone interested in helping, please let Mike know. The
class is projected to have 12-18 students. The location for the classes will be at the
Mormon Church at 4618 Henwick Lane from roughly 9am to 4pm. An exam will be
scheduled sometime after the last class.

A General class may be on the horizon for later in the fall.

will investigate as well.
