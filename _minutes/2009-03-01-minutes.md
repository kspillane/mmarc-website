Digipeater report: NØSS has located a replacement radio. NØKSF currently has it and

Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Channel 2 is still on the air. However,
Roger Volk, KØGOB, has successfully connected to the Centralia node from St. Louis.

Emergency Coordinator's report: NØVAU reported online courses for Level 1, Level 2,
and Level 3 emergency communications classes are available. Examiners are needed in
this area. Four to five people have already applied to be examiners.

No changes in the antenna situation at the State Emergency Management Agency (SEMA)
were noted.

CW Class: The Slow Speed class is continuing. There are currently about 6-7 students
each week. Everyone is having fun. New students are still welcome to attend. This class
meets after breakfast at 9am.

The Advanced Class is continuing as well with participants also having fun and sometimes
actually work on code practice. This class meets before breakfast at 6:45am. Both classes
meet every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café.

Department of Health & Senior Services (DHSS) Class: The class has finished and the
following testing session yielded 15 new licenses including 2 new Generals.

Storm Spotting Class: Several members attended the various classes

NØVAU has three Skywarn bumper stickers for certified storm spotters available for $5
each.

  th

Training Committee: KMØS reported that the first Training Committee meeting was held
during the Chili Cook-off. The General upgrade classes will begin to meet on Thursday
nights beginning March 26th. Sessions will continue through the end of May with the
exception of club meeting nights. The start times will be somewhere between 6:30 to
7:00PM with a firm start time to be determined. The location for the classes will be at the
Department of Health, 930 Wildwood Drive, Jefferson City, MO 65109 in the Maple
Conference Room.

Expenses involved will be the purchase of the General Class License Manual ($19),
General Class Q&A Manual ($14), or both for $33. Also, the cost to take the exam will be
$15 payable at the testing session.

Currently about a dozen people are confirmed to take the course with another 6-10 people
interested.

The classes will consist of 9 sessions lasting roughly 2 to 2-1/2 hours each. Of the 9
sessions, one will be a review/practice exam session, and the actual exam session. In
addition to these, there will be a couple Saturday hands on sessions lasting a couple hours.
  NØSS sent out an email asking for instructors and got a couple responses. If anyone else

  Classes to upgrade to Extra will be planned for later this Fall after school begins. Also,
  plans for another VE instructor class next Spring will be discussed in the future.
