                                      Minutes of the
                              Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.
                                    Jefferson City, MO

Meeting of: August 8, 2024 at the Cole County Sheriff’s Office

Presentation: There was no formal program but several recent Contest and Special Events that
members participated in were discussed.

    •   Spartan Sprint: Kent-K9ZTV discussed his 11 QSO’s during this 2 hour event. Bryan-
        W0ESE also discussed his participation and the station weight requirements used in
        scoring the event. It was noted that conditions were difficult that night due to Radio
        Blackout being present.

    •   Flight of the Bumble Bees: Kent-K9ZTV, Bryan-W0ESE, Rich-N0EAX all discussed
        their participation in this event. Mike-AD0YM was also mentioned as having
        participated in the event. Bad weather brought participation to an end during this 4 hour
        contest. Kent and Bill were noted to be in the top 10 scoring at the time of their entry of
        results. Kent encouraged others to participate in events like these as many of operators
        operate at slow CW speeds.

    •   IARU Contest: Tim-N0UI discussed the participation in this 24 hour event using
        NU1AW/0 as their callsign. Tim and Jim-N0LBY participated from Jim’s place in
        Osage County while Mitch-NW0M participated from his QTH in Jefferson City. The
        three operators were part of an organized Missouri effort with 8 stations operating 8
        band modes. The group was linked together with computers. The Missouri group
        totaled 5100 contacts and were in 20th place worldwide and 2nd in the US. Art-KD0GY
        also participated using the FT8 mode. This was the first time that the callsign NU1AW
        had been used to operate a digital mode. Thunderstorms and partial Radio Blackout
        conditions were evident during the event.

    •   Skeeter Hunt: The members were reminded of the upcoming Skeeter Hunt event on
        August 18.

    •   Night of Nights: Tim-N0UI, Bryan-W0ESE, and Kent-K9ZTV explained the operation
        of retired Coast Guard Station KPH and their amateur radio station K6KPH. They
        discussed their reception of KPH as well as their contracts with K6KPH. Night of Nights
        on July 12, 2024 was a celebration of the 25th Anniversary of the retirement of the Coast
        Guard transmission of the last commercial Morse Code message sent in the US on July
        12, 1999. The amateur radio volunteer organization Maritime Radio Historical Society
        (MRHS) was formed a year later operating the first annual Night of Night event on July
        12, 2000. MRHS operates and maintains the original equipment and antennas. MidMo
        ARC members have participated in this event most years by listening for KPH or
        contacting K6KPH. Several MMARC club members donate to MRHS to maintain the
        equipment and antennas located at Point Reyes National Seashore in California.
        Amateur Radio Station K6KPH also operates on Saturdays.
