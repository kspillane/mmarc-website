CW Class: Kent Trimble – K9ZTV 
 Class continues.  Spent last two sessions practicing for Straight Key Night (SKN). 
 Discussed SKN.  Kent had 7 contacts.   
 Brief explanation of Straight Key Night.  Operating event during New Years Day (Zulu 
Time.)  Operating with a straight key highly encouraged.  Also encouraged to get out 
older radios to operate. 
 4 contacts by Bill Gerth.  Two were made with W9ABD, Callsign for Willis Corwin, 1st 
Jefferson City Amateur Radio Operator. 
 SKN is a wonderful celebration of Amateur Radio heritage.  
 Discussion about operating practices with older radios, and using the lock button to hold 
your frequency.
