                               Minutes of the
                       Mid-MO Amateur Radio Club, Inc.
                             Jefferson City, MO




Meeting of:  March 8, 2001


Meeting called to order by Kurt Bleich (KBØHNR)

In attendance:  KBØHNR, WNØX, KCØACQ, NØKSF, WØJES, KD4FEA, NØRJE, KBØQHZ,
KBØNXK, KCØCZI, KBØOUX, NØIB, KØWYN, NØVGX, WØRAJ, NØSS, WBØVHG, WBØWAP,
NØMV, NØYSE, KØDXR, KØEMT, NØOFD, KØQCX, KØUWM, KCØIFL, KØJJS, KAØOUV,
KCØIQX, NØTKN, KAØYRT, NMØX, K9ZTV.

Date/location of the next meeting:  April 12, 2001.  Central United Church
of Christ, corner of Washington and W. Ashley Streets.   Enter from the
alley on the east side of the church (midway up the alley).

Minutes:  March 8, 2001 minutes reviewed, motion to accept by NØOFD, and
approved.

Bank balance a/o March 8, 2001 is $1, 935.76

Discussion about current list of members and who owes dues was generated by
report of dues collected during February.  KCØACQ will bring current
membership list to the next meeting and will have available at future
meetings in preparation for Field Day.

Old business:

2 Meter Net Report:    12
                 13
                 0
                 12
                 37


No Activity to report on the CW Net.

2 Meter Repeater Report:  Amplifier is at NØSS’s QTH.  Needs to re-build
    it.  Running about 28 – 29 Watts output.  TR Relay stuck in transmit
    mode, getting 1 Watt out, switch was burned out and needs to be re-
    built.

Digi-peater Report:  Looked at the digi-peater but the rubber feet were
    decomposing.  It is working, changed information section, thinking
    about putting it on APRS frequencies.  Testing it at Kurt’s (KBØHNR)
    house.  Has been changed to work better with Kantronics TNC equipment.
    145.09 is covered by a digi-peater in Ashland, so that may be able to
    handle conventional packet traffic.






Emergency Coordinator Report:  None to report, but the severe weather
season is fast-approaching.  Feb 24th  was a weather net and discussion of
how the active traffic was handled.  March 13th is the annual tornado drill
day.  There will be a quick net and report number of participants to NWS
offices in St. Louis and test out the link system.  ARES activity in
Springfield is setting up a system based on the Arkansas state-wide packet
model.  Kevin (KCØCZI) asks that anyone with a weather spotter number that
is also interested in being a ARES member, should provide their NWS spotter
number to him for his records.

Earth Day Special Event:  Reported by Bryan (KØEMT)--  the call sign KØC
has been issued.  We are getting the space at the capitol grounds assigned
to us.  We are still looking for available operators and help with setting
up and tearing down the tents and equipment.  Hours are between 10:00a.m. –
3:30p.m. for the public, but we need time to set up before and after.  Told
the committee of our special needs.  Can not use stakes in the ground, so
that somewhat limits what we can do.  KCØCZI offered his company’s tent.
We should target our message to the 5th grade level and focus on SkyWarn
activities.  Earth Day is Friday April 20th, 2001.

RDF Kit Update:  Eric (NMØX) brought some examples to look at after the
    meeting.


State Fair Representation:  Jack (WØJES) was at SEMA Conference in February
and spoke with Greg Hibbard (WØENW), director of Pettis County EMA, who
said that they no longer set up a ham radio display at the State Fair.
Jack thinks that Mo Dept of Agriculture would allow us to do that and has
submitted a formal proposal.  More to follow next month…

New business:

Tom has 4 truck loads of 1-5/8” hardline stored at his house.  He has to
give it away to anyone that can use it.  It needs to be inventoried and
sorted by lengths.  Could be used to make a 6 Meter duplexer.  Connectors
are expensive, so getting used ones would be a good idea.

Mid Mo SkyWarn Affiliation Letter:  Presented to MMARC for signature,
motion made and seconded to endorse.

KCØCZI reported on the NWS training held on March 1, 2001.  There were
approximately 48 in attendance.  There will be a meeting March 15th to
review spotter information.  Requested to be notified when new licensees
are granted, so that they may receive a packet of information about ARES
and SkyWarn.

 Rohn Tower is being taken down this summer.  100 pounds per section.  Need
to make a trip to Nebraska to get it.

Kent (K9ZTV) will do next month’s presentation.  Will show “The Last Word
Out of Kuwait” about the Gulf War and amateur radio.

Show and Tell of some new equipment after the meeting.

NØXPD is studying for General Class exam and needs a current manual.

Rich Beckwith (WNØX) will get letters out to SEMA and Maytag for Field Day
activities.

2 Meter GP-15 for $135.00, or Comet for $129.00 for club use at Field Day
and MS 150, Boy Scout Jamboree.  Motion made and seconded, voted to
approve, to purchase the GP-15.




Respectfully submitted (04/24/2001): Steven C. Klenc, KCØACQ, Secretary-
Treasurer
