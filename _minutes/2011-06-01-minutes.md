Digipeater report: Rich, NØJBF, reported that the digipeater is ready to be placed back at
the site when Rick finds the time.


Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Mike, WAØZOG, reported on behalf of
Jim, NØTKN, that the system configuration was changed as follows: 1) The local MJEF
configuration was changed to function as a digipeater. 2) OUTPOST software was installed
on the SEMA computer. 3) A borrowed Kantronics KPC3+ was installed at SEMA.

The following recommendations were made: 1) Keep the digipeater function turned on. 2)
SEMA should install and purchase a Kantronics KPC3+. 3) Jefferson City area hams
should consider downloading and installing OUTPOST and practice sending messages.


Emergency Coordinator's report: Rich, NØJBF, reported that the National Level Exercise
provided good training, but not enough operators were available due to other commitments.
Comments were similar to those after the January exercise. The exercise went well, but
some training issues remain including making periodic breaks in the traffic being sent.

J.D., WAØBER, added that the execution of the exercise left a lot to be desired. However,
the radio portion of the exercise was the best part.

Kent, K9ZTV, reported that he and Bill, W4RK, operated from Branson during the exercise.
They passed traffic from that area back to Jefferson City for the Missouri Funeral Director’s
Association. Messages were passed through the National Traffic System successfully.
Kent added that the Net Control Station needs to be in Jefferson City and put this note into
his after-action report.


VE Liaison’s report: Nancie, NMØC, reported one exam to give after the meeting. The
test at the Red Cross facility the past Tuesday evening resulted in seven new General class
licensees.


CW Classes: Andy, WNØI, reported that the advanced class has had a sparse turnout.
More students should start coming to the early class. This class meets at Mel’s Country
Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings.

Kent, K9ZTV, reported that the intermediate class is working on Field Day exchanges. He
added that even if the instructors don’t show up, class members are still encouraged to
meet and work together on their CW skills. The intermediate class meets at Mel’s after
breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am, while the beginning class meets an hour later at
10am.


Training Committee: Nancie, NMØC, reported that the there is a plan to start a VE class
later in the summer.
  Club Shirts/Jackets/Patches: No updates.


  Grant Money: Mike, NØZH, provided Mike Miller with a copy of the Callaway Amateur
  Radio League’s By-Laws for their emergency communications club. The regular club By-
  Laws are hoped to be presented soon. Steve, NNØB, offered to check on getting a copy of
  the By-Laws for the radio club he is affiliated with in New Mexico as well.


  Field Day: Rich, NØJBF, reported that plans for Field Day ran into a stumbling block. The
  MoDOT trailer will not be available as planned and Rick, KCØPET, also will not be available
  due to being re-tasked to deal with flooding issues along the Missouri River.

  The request for the MEOC from the CCERT team has stalled and no answer is yet available
  regarding if the vehicle and tent will be available. Other vehicles that may have been
  available on a contingency basis are no longer available either.

  Several discussions followed ranging from using tents or vehicles from local rental agencies
  to purchasing our own screened canopies in lieu of tents to setting up in one of the parks in
  town. In the end it was decided that Rich would check on the availability of operating at
  McClung Park and report back at the meeting on the Thursday before Field Day.

  Notifications will be sent via email as soon as arrangements for the pre-Field Day meeting
  are finalized. If anyone is not receiving the emails sent out by Mark, please make sure your
  current email address is on file with him.
