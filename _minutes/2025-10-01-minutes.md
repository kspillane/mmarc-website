   Cole County Emergency Communications Exercise.
         o JD and Rich are not here. Information is being distributed via e-mail.
   Osage County Emergency Communications Plan
         o Brief review of history and current situation in Osage County
         o Previous station had been assembled, then was torn apart.
         o Discussed with resurrecting the plan.
         o Plan includes using FRS, GMRS, and other radio systems to ensure
             communications are available from the widest group of people in the county.
         o Idea is to have different layers in the overlay model that can get the information to
             where it needs to get.
         o Sheriff wanted to be able to send resources to correct place in an emergency.
         o People with the right radios can get the information all the way to MOSWIN.
         o Amateur Radio is still a key part of the plan. We are responsible to teach and
             demonstrate how to communicate to the other downline layers.
         o Important to ensure ARRL continues to push in the right direction, and welcome
             all.
         o Additional discussion about licensing in the different disciplines, and other
             challenges still to address.
