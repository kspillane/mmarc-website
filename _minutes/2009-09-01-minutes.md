2-Meter Repeater report: New modem has not yet been installed. Tim and Tom are
hoping to get the repeater pulled and do testing. The repeater may be down for a day or
two during this time. An email will be circulated to notify when this takes place.
Digipeater report: No report.

Missouri Emergency Packet Network (MEPN): Jim, NØTKN, reported that more field
trips were made to the site and a bad modem was identified. A replacement modem has
been acquired and another trip will take place to install it next week. The last replacement
modem lasted about 2 days.

Bill, W4RK, reported that he and Jim were able to check into the St. Louis County ARES
digital net on MEPN. The link was made going direct to Centralia and skipping the
Jefferson City node.

Emergency Coordinator's report: Joe, KBØQHZ reported nothing major this month so
far. There is some coordination being done for the communications exercise on October 7th
from 9:00AM to 2:30PM. The MO Emergency Services Net will be activated on 3.963 MHz
with an alternate of 7.263 MHz for the entirety of the SEMA exercise. It is hoped to have 4
to 5 local HF capable stations with Internet available in the shack to help.
                                                      nd
There will be E TEAM training held on September 22 from 6:00PM to 10:00PM for those
interested in participating in the exercise on October 7th.

Rick, KCØPET, reported the Level 1 EMCOMM classes have been narrowed down to two
days, October 3rd and October 10th from 10:30AM to 5:30PM. The test date is not yet set.

VE Liaison’s report: Jack, WBØS, reported that the test session after the last meeting
resulted in one new Technician licensee, KDØIRG. No tests are currently scheduled.

CW Class: Kent, K9ZTV, reports that they are continuing to lean on the students to get
them to higher speeds. They are up to 15wpm without using the Farnsworth method. A
new class may be started if there is enough interest from new students. This class meets
after breakfast at 9am every Saturday morning at Mel’s Country Café.

Tom, NØSS, reports that the advanced class is still going well. This class meets before
breakfast at Mel’s at 6:45am.

Training Committee: Mike, KMØS, reported that the committee is working on putting
together an Extra study session and would like people to think about dates and times to
start. It will be a Q&A format with discussions. Technician classes are being held by state
agencies but if the club would like to do one also, it is something to think about. A General
study session will be looked into for next Spring.
