   •   Kent reported “Perfect” for the class. Kent also indicated that the class has been and
       would continue to be practicing with straight keys in anticipation of Straight Key Night
       (SKN). SKN will begin at 6:00 pm CT on New Year’s Eve and continue for 24 hours. A
       discussion of old keys, old chirpy rigs, and old operators took place indicating what
       would be heard on the air during SKN. All were encouraged to get on the air or at least
       listen in for some fun.

New Business:

   •   Tim indicated that Presentations are needed for the upcoming 2025 year.
  •   Steve-WW0G indicated that he was headed to Belize for his seventh year to join former
      club member John Patterson in operating John’s contest station during the February
      ARRL DX CW contest. The callsign V3T has been used in recent years and many club
      members contact that station during the February event giving V3T additional points.

  •   David- W0WWM reminded club members that the National Weather Service (NWS) has
      updated their 2025 training schedule. Tim showed the NWS website with a virtual
      Weather Spotter training class scheduled for January 30. Kent reminded club members
      that the training changes and club members are encouraged to frequently take the class.
      Local sessions (not on-line) are frequently scheduled also. David also mentioned that
      class members are given the NWS St. Louis phone number to call to report appropriate
      weather changes during a weather event.

  •   Tim thanked everyone for the support during his two years as the President and for their
      support of club activities such as Field Day and Frankenstein operations. Tim received a
      nice round of applause for his efforts. Thanks Tim!
