              th
net on the 11 , while operating from JCPD, simplex reception was checked to get an idea of

and out to the west near the McGirk area.

Joe suggested that those calling the net, request signal reports from stations to see how well
they can copy Net Control on simplex. This will give you a good idea of how well your signal
can be copied from your location should the repeater be unavailable.
