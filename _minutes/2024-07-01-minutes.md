Meeting of: July 11, 2023 at the Cole County Emergency Management Office

Presentation:

    •   Jason Piper, KF0QIL presented information on Meshtastic, a no license required
        communication mode. Meshtastic is an open source, off-grid, decentralized, mesh
        network built to run on affordable, low-power devices on the 33cm band. Ham modes
        and no license modes are available on the radios. Jason showed examples of his
        Meshtastic radios. Thanks Jason for the presentation.

    •   Field Day- Bryan, W0ESE reported that there were approximately 1100 contacts made
        by MMARC operators. This was more than were reported over the last several years
        except for 2020. The total number of points was 4,898. Bryan reviewed all of the bonus
        categories and the points that the club took credit for. He also related the experiences
        that he noticed in others who operated the event.
