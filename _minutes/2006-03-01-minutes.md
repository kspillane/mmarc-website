  2-meter Repeater report: KAØOUV reported that the new repeater is ready to be installed

  be kept in place as a stand-by, backup repeater. They haven’t decided yet whether the

  Emergency Coordinators report: Volunteers are needed for the May 20, 2006 Easter
  Seal Walkathon.

  CW Class: The CW class continues to meet every Saturday, at 0645 (before breakfast), at
  Mel’s Country Café.

New business:

  Dues: Jack Boswell (NØKSF) noted that many members are late with their annual dues
  payment, and suggested that a general email message be sent to all members by Tom
  Hammond (NØSS) reminding them that the dues are due.

  Chili Cook off: More than 30 people showed up and all had a good time. Many thanks
  were expressed to Tom (NØSS) and Jeri (KØRPH) Hammond for hosting the occasion.

  Dayton: Kent Trimble (K9ZTV) has two rooms reserved, if anyone needs them. Four Days
  In May (FDIM) will be at the Fairborn Holiday Inn, same as last year. Kent encouraged
  everybody to go and support the Hamfest.

  Field Day: Mike Miller (WAØZOG) reported that Gary Zielman (KØWYN), who was not
  present, has suggested sponsoring a Boy Scout Merit Badge to attract the public. After
  some discussion, it was suggested that Jim Kovar (KCØHOS), who has worked extensively
  with the Boy Scouts, be asked to be the BSA contact. Mike Miller (WAØZOG)
  recommended the use of trailers, air conditioning, and 100 watt power this year, and Kent
  Trimble (K9ZTV) reminded everyone of the sunspot minimum and recommended using the
  Tri-Bander. Charles Golden (NØROK) recommended the lot in front of the Capital Mall as
  this year’s location, and Ben Hoffman (NØOFD) recommended the Porite location as first
  choice. A consensus vote was held and Porite and 100 watts were the result.

  DX and Diving: Kevin Brown (KCØCZI) reported on his diving vacation to Bonaire. He was
  only able to operate on two occasions, and didn’t get out very well, but he won some money
  on a bet.

  Presentation: Jack Boswell (NØKSF) presented a program on the use of the MFJ 259-B
  Antenna Analyzer.

VE exams: Mike McCrady (KCØUZA) took and passed his code test and became a General
Class Operator. Owen Toalson (KCØLZG) took and passed the code test and became a
Technician Plus Class Operator. Mike Thayer took and passed the Technician exam to
become a Technician Class Operator.

Dues received at (or since) meeting: $50.00
