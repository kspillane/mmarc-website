Field Day: Rich, NØJBF, reported that Field Day went well despite a very bumpy road
getting started. After vehicle shortages, a lightning storm, bad coax, and computer
interfacing failures to name a few things, the stations were finally fully up and running by
around 1:30pm. The unofficial score is in the 4,000-point range. Totals were roughly 380
contacts on VHF, 600 on CW, 160 on SSB, and 60 on GOTA.

Recommendations for the future include working up an operations manual. This would have
instructions on how to set up the antennas, showing which coax to use, how to interface the
computers, etc. Examples of such manuals from other organizations will be provided so that
we can create one of our own.
New business

Mike, WAØZOG, noted that two cross-band repeaters were recently donated to the club. They
are Kenwood commercial repeaters that we could choose to reconfigure for use in the amateur
bands and use as possible remote sites to improve our coverage area. We should be thinking
of ways to best utilize these items. On a side note, it was proposed that we look to add some
new blood to the Technical Committee. Possible candidates include Jim, NØTKN; Jack,
NØKSF; Owen, KCØLZG; and Dan, WAØTIC.
