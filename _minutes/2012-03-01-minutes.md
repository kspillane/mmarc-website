2-Meter Repeater report: Jim, NØTKN, reported that the repeater is functioning. The
phone line has been repaired and the autopatch now functions correctly. Before purchasing
a new battery Jim took a known good battery to the repeater site and hooked it up. The test
revealed that during a power failure, the repeater would reset itself upon changeover to
battery power. So simply purchasing a battery and installing it will not solve the reset issue.
Also, the repeater will not charge the battery.

Methods to solve these issues were investigated and included: Option A – Purchase an
Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) to keep the repeater powered up during short power
outages. While this will keep the repeater from resetting, it would only be useful for short
durations before shutting down. Price: around $300. Option B – Purchase the new battery
system which would help keep power available for longer durations (up to a couple days), but
still have the annoying reset issue. Price: around $500-600. And finally, Option C – purchase
both the UPS and a new battery system to be used in conjunction. Price: around $900.

Joe, KBØQHZ, moved that the club pursue Option C and Bill, W4RK, seconded. A short
discussion took place and the motion passed.
