activity due to other events that have been taking place such as hamfests, JOTA, etc. Once
the Technician classes have wrapped up, the 10M net on Monday nights will hopefully pick
back up as well. The net is held on 28.100Mhz at 7pm local time. Any speeds are welcome.

Bill, W4RK, added that the 4 States QRP Group has CW nets that take place on Wednesday
evenings on 3.564 and 7.122Mhz at 7:00, 7:30, 8:00, and 8:30pm.

The advanced class meets at Mel’s Country Café at 6:45am on Saturday mornings. The
intermediate class meets at Mel’s after breakfast on Saturday mornings at 9am. The
beginning class meets at 10am.

Announcements:

Kent, K9ZTV, announced that he has received some QSL cards from the JOTA operation for
NØSS which is now the club’s special event callsign. We currently do not have any QSL cards
though. Kent will contact Karen Pettite about coming up with a QSL card design. In the
meantime, input from club members would be appreciated in regards to address on the cards,
layouts, images, etc. Please think about this and let Kent know if you have any ideas for a
design. Several designs will then be created and the club will vote on the final card design.


Kent, K9ZTV, also announced that he has received several QSL cards from the QSL Bureau for
