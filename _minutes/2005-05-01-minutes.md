MEPN 6 Meter Node: Centrallia node delayed due to health issues of climber. New climber
hired to start after Dayton.



MS-150: Volunteers are need for 25 sag wagons. A new 40-mile course has been added this
year. Training for operators will be available during a club meeting. Use of APRS discussed.


New Business:


Field Day, 2005: The club was presented with a field day proposal (prepared by Kent).
Highlights of the proposal include:

   Using The N.J.C.R.A. site
   Use of wire antennas only (except VHF/UHF station)
   Stations will operate QRP from battery power
   Stations will be housed in tents
   Emphasis will be on having fun scoring of least importance

The club discussed the proposal and it was generally accepted.

Member news: Dave Taylor (NØMV) announced he was moving out of state. Dave is a good
friend and an asset to the club. The club is excited for Dave, but he will be missed.
