Tech Group:

      Jack Boswell highlighted the visit to this meeting of club member Bryan Nehl,
       K0EMT who is visiting from Indiana. Bryan brought some circuit boards for a
       QRP antenna matching project. Jack discussed his purchase of the T41 SDR
       Transceiver kit that he has purchased. He explained several of the details of the
       construction including that surface mount components have been pre-installed.

Old Business:

      Moody Estate Sale: Kent thanked the club members for the excellent help in the
       purchase of estate sale items. Purchases at the Moody house brought $2,523 with
       Collinsville purchases at $510. Expenses incurred were $96 for a total given to the
       family of $2,937. Jim Biggerstaff also thanked everyone for their purchases. Jim
       and Kent were thanked for their efforts at the sale. Jim Branson expressed a desire
       to sell the antennas to the club members. He will use Group I/O first to list the
       antennas after obtaining permission from the estate administrator. He will then
       use other avenues of listing the antennas for sale. Some of the antenna details were
       discussed.

      Estate Sale Policy: The MMARC Board met yesterday, February 7, 2024, and
       discussed the draft policy that had been prepared by Kent. These guidelines will be
       discussed again at the next Board meeting on February 19, 2024. A question
       about the 501.C3 status of the club was discussed. Kent identified that the club is
       considered a “hobby club” and not a 501.C3 organization. It was recommended
       that the guideline might include language about the 501.C3 status. The point was
       to remind club members that they might tell those requesting a sale that a donation
       to the club had no tax consequences.
