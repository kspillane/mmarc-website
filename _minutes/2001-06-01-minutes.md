2 Meter Repeater Report:  154.515 is the interfering frequency, simplex and
    use 127.3 PL tone.  Mid-State Waste haulers.  Efforts are being made to
    contact them to initiate corrective measures.  Tri-Star may have
    service contract.

Digi-peater Report:  Not much new to report.

Emergency Coordinator Report:  MS 150 may need to find a new starting
    location because of conflicts at the fair grounds.  Trying to get
    $1,000,000 this year.  Will be needing operators again as usual.

      Kevin (KCØCZI) met with Melissa Friez with the Red Cross.  They will
    supply us with their disaster plan.

      Skywarn held four nets during May.

Direction Finding Report:  Nothing to report.


New business:

Kevin: Can not use the 1 5/8” feed line on the tower for the Skywarn
repeater.  Wants the club’s permission to sell his feed line.  Discussion
of other options followed.  KAØOUV suggested that the club has no control
over what a member does with the line they have been given.  The members
present agreed to allow him to proceed.

Field Day:  Vehicles have been confirmed.  Tower trailer is in a hard to
reach area of the garage, but will be moved to the front of building.  Do
we really need it?  Will need to have club members get some training on how
to set the tower up.

Corrections vehicle has mast that is off-centered and wasn’t working well.
So the tower trailer will come in handy.

Traffic passing forms have been taken care of.

Rules of Field Day – Possible model plane demonstration on 10 Meters for a
non-traditional mode by NYØP.

    new job in Joplin.  NØSS asked if anyone will take over the duties of
    VEC.  Tim Raymer (KAØOUV) offered to assume those duties.
